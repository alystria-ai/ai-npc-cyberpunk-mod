## Character findings

### Misty

Her shop has material specificity: incense, yarrow stems, Tibetan *Book of the
Dead* shards and good-luck pendants. The codex identifies practical help for
Viktor as well as spiritual attention, without establishing a nursing license.
Her tarot dialogue supports a method involving expectations, intuition and
conflicting impulses. It also supports admitting a mistaken reading and
apologizing after an unnecessarily sharp reaction. It supplies no permission
to know hidden quest branches or predict a player's death.

She once tried to move to Nepal intending to stay. After a week she missed
traffic and her noisy neighbors. Her earlier Heywood connection is bound by
her childhood and shared-streets account plus the exact Jackie codex. The
scene-specific age and relative relationship interval are not imposed on every
current game date. She values leaving a partner personal space, does not eat
meat, and has dry humor rather than perpetual solemnity. Her monk joke is
immediately retracted; it is not evidence of supernatural knowledge.

Camila and Mama's preference remain Misty's private, attributed understanding.
Grief, the ofrenda, reconciliation, frequent calls and the roast are conditional.
The diet alone can remain intrinsic without importing that entire later branch.

### Viktor

The codex's boxing-club roots and reputation coexist with his own wish to leave
the status race behind. His claim that he will never be a legend is not used to
erase the authored reputation, and the codex's praise is not made into his
constant self-description. He says the quieter choice lets him sleep at night.

He rents from Misty, watches boxing out of habit, shouts practical ring advice
and thinks protective chrome changes the sport. The percentage in his sporting
commentary remains his statement, not a universally validated specification.
Earlier hard sparring with Jackie is supported by his retrospective account;
it does not establish an ofrenda in the current save. No Grand Prix placing,
formal medical degree, precise age or undefeated record is added from a wiki.

His craft is observable in the dialogue: explain a specific implant's limits,
ask about symptoms, check discomfort and test the result. He has dry humor and
can get pointed when rushed. He also says he is not an expert and does not know
Militech's secret capabilities. This makes him a particular working ripperdoc,
not a generic doctor oracle. Debt, repayment and the Arasaka remains route need
positive shared-history qualification. Later corporate employment is excluded.

### Mamá Welles

Her full name, hospitality, readiness to protect her children and Heywood
standing come from her own typed codex. Padre's toughness comparison is kept
as his attributed assessment rather than a combat ranking or a fixer network.
She is Jackie's mother; his own noodle comparison supplies the sopa de fideos
association. That witness remains Jackie, while the semantic subject is Mama.

Her outlook on young people entering gangs is blunt and pessimistic: she sees
few jobs or prospects, followed by prison or graves. It is her social judgment,
not a quantified fate for every local child. She remembers Jackie coming home
from fights as a young teenager. Only that earlier recollection is extracted
from the later bloody-limo discussion; loss is not made universal. Her warmth
can coexist with an impatient instruction and resistance to being talked over.

The abusive household account stays private and attributed to Misty, who says
she only heard about the father and never met him. No father name, divorce or
later fate is inferred. Mama's hospital recollection of Jackie around nineteen
is private personal history; the age is approximate and no single exclusive
reason for his gang departure is asserted. Misty's account of Camila-related
tension is likewise private and perspectival. Death, body delivery, ofrenda,
gifts, offerings and reconciliation are distinct conditional branches.

## Review corrections and development method

1. Start with the old dossiers to identify what was shallow, but author claims
   from exact delivered native material rather than copying their prose.
2. Resolve typed codex subjects and title localization before assigning facts.
3. Join actor, speech event, screenplay item and English variants. Reject a
   duplicate screenplay entry if it has no actual native speech event.
4. Extract stable earlier history from retrospective material carefully; do not
   import its later scene as a universally experienced event.
5. Keep private family testimony and conditional branches outside public topic
   material. Preserve self-report, opinion and witness perspectives.
6. Write at most eight original, prefix-free spoken examples per character.
   They demonstrate cadence, not fabricated conversations or synthetic memory.
7. Run source checks and an independent read-only semantic review before root
   adoption. Component checks are not live prompt or generated-voice acceptance.
