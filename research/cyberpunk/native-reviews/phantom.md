## Authoring and privacy review

Static biographies describe established identity and earlier history. Future succession, quest completions, relationships and careers are conditional. Each conditional dossier reference has a specific decision ID and outcome key.

Source disagreement and gaps remain explicit, including Bill's relationship terminology and surname spelling, Nele's uncertain off-screen fate, a less documented Jago succession alternative, and the distinction between Aaron Waines and Aaron McCarlson. Paco's Deep Dive retelling, Nele's account of intent, and Steven's political rationalization are attributed claims rather than omniscient narration.

The audit corrected these potential leaks:

- Songbird's relationship baseline no longer presumes the aircraft operation already failed.
- Hands' Bennett/Jago baselines describe existing offices and interests, not an already opened succession contest.
- Bill's roster-key implementation note was removed from the biography.
- Songbird does not automatically hear V's private report to Reed.

- Aaron is unconscious during the Angie negotiation. His later direct conversation and messages are different evidence.
- Briana initially receives a death report about Rinder, which can be false. The later survival discovery is separately conditional.
- A fixer knowing a job's client does not establish knowledge of every private encounter within that job.

## Evidence handling

- Scene descriptors select localeId=en_us by value, not by a presumed array index.
- locstringId joins to the scene screenplay item and vpeIndex joins to the payload.
- Selected lines also cite the matching shipped English localization asset where present.
- The audit of the supplied batches found 40 scene-versus-shipped differences and ten absent IDs in phantom-native, two differences/one absent ID in phantom-nele, one difference/no absent IDs in phantom-aaron, and one difference/three absent IDs in phantom-final. Not all lines are used by this pack. These differences must be reviewed as content, not ignored because the scene exports compiled.
- The decisive choice meanings used here survived the comparison; displayed punctuation and wording can differ. The follow-up Aaron messages cite the shipped onscreen records directly.
- Winning base/EP1 inventory entries are retained by original path. Raw binary SHA256 and pointer/type/value checks prevent a superseded asset or a stale arbitrary path from serving as proof.
- Source facts, normal entry/output wiring, scene participation and final-stage gates were inspected together. Asset membership alone does not prove an executed branch.
- Positive exact writes qualify observations; absent/zero flags do not select an alternate story.
