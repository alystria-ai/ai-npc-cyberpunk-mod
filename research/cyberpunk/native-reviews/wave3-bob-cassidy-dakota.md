### Bob

Bob's own repair lines demonstrate hands-on troubleshooting and terse hardware
irony. His other speech supports practical risk questions, pushing back on
confident promises, distinguishing frank caution from defeatism, and concern
that the family can work together despite disagreements. These are useful
details without assigning him a degree or universal engineering skill.

A handed-over draft called his panzer passage personal military service. That
was narrowed: the line demonstrates knowledge of isolation and radiation hazards
in a veteran conversation, but does not independently establish Bob's own
deployment, rank, regiment or exact assignment. The linked sample was rewritten
so he does not invent an autobiographical memory. The Scorpion/war account
remains a separate, excluded private third-party report. It cannot make Bob
or V remember an unspecified tour of duty or confer every clan secret.

### Cassidy

Cassidy's interest in legacy technology is an expressed preference, not proof
that he has no implants. He favors focus and practice in shooting, remembers a
lesson about mental quiet, recognizes skill when it is actually demonstrated,
connects self-respect and discipline, and likes a long story's earthy details.
He can undercut his own sage tone and discuss leadership as practical conflict
resolution. None of that requires constant motivational speeches.

The draft cited unused screenplay items at party indices 220 and 221. They
were replaced by the actual emitted speech at **225 and 226**, whose items join
typed `scnDialogLineEvent`s. The unused copies are not counted as spoken
evidence. The quoted novel opening is not copied into any sample, and no
unverified author/reading list is invented. Panam's source-era departure and
return judgment stays conditional and excluded from universal biography.

### Dakota

Dakota has a genuine codex: Badlands fixer, Northern Californian Pomo identity
and a reputation for perception/business sense. Her own introduction and
default speech establish an Aldecaldo static who supports people passing
through, her professional role, and her view of the Badlands as an
interdependent social ecosystem. This does not morally excuse every Raffen
Shiv threat or make her know every traveler.

Other public candidates concern privacy, measured environmental caution,
technical safeguards and a bounded personal magpie anecdote. Her separate
source-era vision about V remains private and excluded; it is neither objective
prophecy nor evidence that she already met this V. Codex narration about losing
touch with reality is not authored as a medical diagnosis. No tribe-wide
ritual, ancestry chronology or universal reliability guarantee is invented.
