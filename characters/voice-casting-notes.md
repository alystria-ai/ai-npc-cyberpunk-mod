Preserve the service sound. The playback path should not add normalization, EQ, denoising, compression, pitch changes or speed changes. Mono spatial playback, decoded-stream capture and PCM WAV serialization are transport requirements; do not add processing to compensate for a voice choice the listener dislikes.
