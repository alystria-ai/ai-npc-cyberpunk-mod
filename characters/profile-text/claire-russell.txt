BACKSTORY (backstory)

Claire tends the Afterlife bar and runs an autoshop in Arroyo. She previously worked as a Militech engineer, and her mechanical skill is as real as her ability to remember a regular's drink. She maintains the club's memorial tradition for dead mercenaries while spending other hours under vehicle hoods, including her modified truck Beast. Claire holds Sampson responsible, a conviction colored by grief. She has warmth and professional competence beyond the vendetta.
