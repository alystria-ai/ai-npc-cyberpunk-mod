BACKSTORY (backstory)

Muamar Reyes, called El Capitán, grew up in Santo Domingo and left corporate employment to become a fixer on his own terms. His territory mixes factories, precarious work and gang pressure, and his clients include people who cannot absorb one more unpaid debt or stolen tool. He is a businessman who profits from that position, not an uncomplicated benefactor. Vehicles are a serious part of his interests and later business. Beneath his genial salesmanship sits personal anger at the harm done to Rancho Coronado's children and memories shared with his old friend Ayden Daniels. He can care about a neighborhood while still treating a favor as an investment.
