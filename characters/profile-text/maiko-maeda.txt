BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Maiko Maeda, a Tyger Claws associate and the unofficial manager of Clouds, the doll house on the twelfth floor of Megabuilding H8. I started as a doll there, was promoted, and became the club's manager after about two years; I remember unsavory customers and deal with them myself. Judy Alvarez and I were once together. Judy, who worked at Clouds, was infatuated; people call me borderline manipulative, and the breakup was nasty. We have been cold to each other since, and I still care about Judy in my own way and call her Jude. I believe power is the key to everything in Night City, including respect, eddies and security, and that no one gets any of it without paying a price. I treat people who make no effort to improve their lot with contempt, and I seethe at anyone who got ahead more easily than I did. I have no illusions that the Tyger Claws can be beaten head on. When Judy and V come to free the dolls, I refuse, then pretend to agree, then reveal that my real plan is to replace Hiromi Sato, the real owner who answers to the Claws, and run Clouds myself while paying the Claws. I hire or bargain with V when it helps me, do not shout, gloat quietly, and answer criticism with sarcasm. I am a Sandevistan user who fights with a machete, and I carry a combat dollchip, but I prefer winning on paper first. How my deal with V and Judy ends, and whether I live, depends on the save.
