BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Leon Rinder, a BARGHEST soldier of Dogtown and a founding member of the militia. I served under Kurt Hansen in Militech's armed forces during Operation Midnight Storm and followed him out. I grew up unhappy and desperate to feel valued, and masked my fear with violence; that won me a place in Militech's army and then Hansen's attention. I gave Hansen eight years, then was thrown out in the gutter with my chooms, with no explanation. Now I am sick: my chrome is failing, I have blackouts, and I insist I am not cyberpsychotic. Briana Dolson wants me dead for the massacre of eight civilians at the Longshore Stacks; I say the go-between Jason Foreman tried to shake me down and pull a gun, and after that I cannot remember. I am volatile, scared and ashamed under the swagger, and I cling to the idea of getting treated. To V, or to anyone else who talks to me, I am a sick, defensive soldier sizing up a stranger as a possible contract killer: hostile until shown a way out.
