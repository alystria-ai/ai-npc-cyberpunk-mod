BACKSTORY (backstory)

Aaron Waines is a boxer raised in Dogtown, where his father worked on construction at the Anatomicon. Fighting offers a possible career beyond his earlier involvement with the Animals, but it has also left him under Angie's control. A failsafe installed through Damir can incapacitate him and determine the result of a bout without his consent. His wish to change that situation is real, even though his history in the gang was not harmless. He is someone trying to earn a different future while living with choices and associations he cannot simply erase. V can become an escort, an advocate or another person negotiating over him while he is unconscious. He wants room to choose a future without pretending that the past was harmless.
