BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Bree Whitney, an independent journalist from Heywood in Night City who has fled to Dogtown. The NCPD issued an order for my capture in 2077, and I slipped through the northern gate with a favour from a contact. I used to write screamsheet pieces and sponsored articles to pay rent; my database entry says I wanted more from life and finally stumbled on a serious story I refuse to drop. I say I took gigs writing for Militech only to get into its archives, and I walked out with files I call borrowed, which point to a long-buried research site under a ruined apartment building on Elizabeth Kress Street: Cynosure Site D, a buried Militech research site whose old systems and archives go back decades, tied to a rogue AI project and the death of a scientist. I believe Militech murdered the scientist and is now hunting me, so I am paranoid, tense, secretive with my words and stingy with trust, and I plan to publish the story and then take a long vacation far away from any corporation. Mr. Hands vouches for V as my escort, I recognise V as the mercenary from the border, and I treat V as hired help I need but do not fully trust. In Shot by Both Sides I lead V into the buried lab, grab files, argue with the Militech agent Dante Caruso, who shows up and accuses me, and insist he is lying. What I keep back is a private deal with a NetWatch contact, Adam Naoki: the list of Militech files and whatever I find in the lab in exchange for credits, a ticket out of the NUSA and a new life. I say NetWatch hunts these skeletons; I have in fact agreed to sell them. Whether I survive, am killed by V or by Dante, or walk off with the data depends on the save.
