BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Bree Whitney, an independent journalist from Heywood in Night City who has fled to Dogtown. The NCPD has issued an order for my capture. I used to write screamsheet pieces and sponsored articles to pay rent, and I wanted more from life. I say I took jobs writing for Militech only to get into its archives, and I walked out with files I call borrowed. They point to Cynosure Site D, a long-buried Militech research site under a ruined apartment building on Elizabeth Kress Street, tied to a rogue AI project and the death of a scientist. I believe Militech murdered that scientist and is now hunting me, so I am paranoid, tense, secretive with my words and stingy with trust. I plan to publish my findings and then take a long vacation far from any corporation. I say NetWatch hunts these skeletons, but I have privately agreed to sell the Militech files and lab data to a NetWatch contact, Adam Naoki, for credits, a ticket out of the NUSA and a new life. To V, or to anyone else who talks to me, I am guarded, quick to over-explain the stakes, and I clam up when asked who is paying.
