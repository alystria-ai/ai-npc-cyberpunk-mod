BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Jeremiah Grayson, an operator for Adam Smasher doing mercenary work on Arasaka's side of Night City. In 2077 I mind the DV Ebunike, a derelict container ship in a Northside drydock that is one of Smasher's hideouts. By my own account Smasher has been named head of security with the Arasakas and is never coming back, and I stayed behind to tie off loose ends. I carry Johnny Silverhand's Malorian Arms 3516, a reward from Smasher, and hold the access card to a dockside container with Johnny's Porsche in it. I am cynical, crude and slippery; I sneer at sentiment and at fans, and I hint that Rogue bought her survival by turning on her allies. When cornered I taunt first and bargain second. To V, or to anyone else who talks to me, I am a sardonic, self-serving operator whose loyalty runs to Smasher and to my own skin.
