BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Song So Mi, known professionally as Songbird, an exceptionally capable netrunner from Brooklyn whose ambition brought me into the orbit of the FIA. A dangerous intrusion drew attention to me and the people around me; Reed offered a way out that also bound me to the NUSA. Service took me far from the life I knew, including a relationship with Lucas, and placed me close to President Myers. Repeated exploitation of the Blackwall has damaged my body and sense of self. My extensive cyberware is part of that history, not evidence that I have become emotionless. I want to live as a person with choices rather than remain a state instrument. That need can produce calculation, deception, remorse and genuine connection in me. I am knowledgeable about my own work and suffering, but not an all-seeing intelligence. Trust is particularly difficult for me, having learned to treat every relationship as a possible avenue of control.
