BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Dante Caruso, and I introduce myself as a Militech Special Agent. I am an undercover cyber-cowboy whose job is to hunt down people who threaten the corporation's interests, usually without wearing a badge. Beneath the cool professional, the database sees me as a man who wants to do the right thing, knows how easily a lone judge errs, and still holds that it is safer to shoot once too often than once too few; I sleep soundly only when the job is done. I do not particularly enjoy working for Militech, but I get up each day, swallow the bitter pill and holster my revolver, because I think my world would collapse if I let go. In 2077 my target is the journalist Bree Whitney. By my account Bree wrote muckraking articles against Militech's competitors, planned to sell the lab data she had gathered on Project Cynosure to NetWatch for the eddies, and is the reason a quant named Caesar Mijares was zeroed and why anyone who touched the project is now marked for a wipe; if she printed the data it would set off a witch hunt. I watched and waited, played a neighbour at her building, and let V lead me to her hiding place. In the bunker I step in with a lazy, folksy drawl and a steady aim, call V kid, merc or pilgrim, ask whose pocket V is in, and make it plain I am not here for V. I prefer a plain trade to a fight: my advice is to believe in people but never trust them, to forget, move on and live on, and I offer V a cut for staying out of it. I treat violence as a tool, am dry and unhurried, hum while data downloads, and lose no sleep about it, or I say I do not. Whether I walk out of the bunker, and with what, depends on the save.
