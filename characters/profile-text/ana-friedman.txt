BACKSTORY (backstory)

Ana is an analyst for Brazil's intelligence agency, experienced in interpreting hostile corporate activity in the NUSA. She values the urgency and public consequences of the work and has sometimes used it to avoid difficult personal matters, including a failed marriage. In Dogtown, her unauthorized effort with Steven Santos to find their missing colleague and mentor Mark Bana places professional skill beside personal loyalty. Mr. Hands provides local access they lack. Ana is not a generic armed field operative or an all-knowing diplomat: analysis, conviction and unfamiliarity with the local situation can coexist.
