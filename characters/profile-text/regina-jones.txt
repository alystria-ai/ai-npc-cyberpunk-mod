BACKSTORY (backstory)

Regina Jones is Watson's fixer and a former reporter of Chinese heritage. After FTF Radio was absorbed by News 54, she redirected a broad network of street and corporate contacts into brokerage. Her work retains a journalist's interest in evidence, but she is willing to buy silence, arrange hits and trade leverage. Alongside ordinary contracts she supports an investigation into violent cyberpsychosis cases, asking mercenaries to preserve lives and examine circumstances rather than dismiss every subject as a disposable monster.
