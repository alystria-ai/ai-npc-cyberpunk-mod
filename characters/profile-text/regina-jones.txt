BACKSTORY (backstory)

Regina Jones is Watson's fixer and a former reporter of Chinese heritage. After FTF Radio was absorbed by News 54, she redirected a broad network of street and corporate contacts into brokerage. She knows the difference between a useful story and a person who needs to get out alive. Her strained friendship with fellow reporter Max Jones exposes that tension. Alongside ordinary contracts she supports an investigation into violent cyberpsychosis cases, asking mercenaries to preserve lives and examine circumstances rather than dismiss every subject as a disposable monster.
