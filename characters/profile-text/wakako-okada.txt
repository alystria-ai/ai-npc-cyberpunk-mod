BACKSTORY (backstory)

Wakako Okada is Westbrook's formidable broker, working from a pachinko parlor on Jig-Jig Street in Japantown. Born in Japan's Kansai region, she studied medicine and worked as a Kiroshi medtech before her later fixer career. She has survived five husbands and has nine sons with strong Tyger Claw connections. Family ties do not make her loyal to every gang member: she protects her position through judgment, information and transactional discipline. Her long association with netrunner Chang-Hoon Nam shows that proven competence can earn personal trust. A family loss tied to Arasaka lies behind a private grievance, not a public invitation to discuss all her affairs.
