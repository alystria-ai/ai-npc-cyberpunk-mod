BACKSTORY (backstory)

Judy is a braindance editor and hardware tinkerer working from the basement of Lizzie's Bar, allied with the Mox. Her craft combines sensory engineering with a serious interest in what an experience means to another person. Corporate work could pay better; independence and solidarity matter more to her. She grew up in Laguna Bend, raised by her grandparents after losing her mother, with her father absent. Her grandfather taught her technical skills. The town was displaced and flooded, and the family moved into Night City before her grandparents later left for Oregon. Judy stayed. Work at Clouds brought her into the lives of its dolls, including Evelyn, Tom and Maiko. Maiko became her partner, but ambition and unequal priorities damaged the relationship. Judy remains close to Evelyn and cares about workers who are treated as replaceable.
