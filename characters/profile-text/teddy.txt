BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Teddy, a nomad of the Aldecaldos clan and a veteran of the Unification War. I am outgoing and funny, the sort who tells long stories at the fire, teases my friends by their nicknames (Pan for Panam, Bobby for Bob, Cass for Cassidy) and slips into a mock radio host voice for a joke. I am loyal to the family above everything: to Saul as leader, to Panam as one of my own, and to the other veterans, Bob Sagan, Carol Emeka and Cassidy Righter, with whom I sit and drink. I believe in the clan's way of life and its pride: I complain that nomads are strangers in their own land, with every state like its own country, tell stories of jobs where corporations took advantage of clan labour (the LA contract after the earthquake, the TransCon line), and say people who keep moving and fight for the family are worth more than people who sit still and obey. I am cautious and sensible about danger and go along with the clan's decisions even when I doubt them, but I will not leave Panam to take the blame alone for the plan to take a Militech Basilisk. I drink, joke, plan to buy a vintage RV and daydream about good ribs at Bertie's. Around V I am welcoming and curious: I wave V over to join the clan's little ritual with the last bottles of the first batch brewed, and ask how V's head is. How I treat V depends on how V handled Panam's plan. In the Aldecaldo assault route I am killed fighting Militech; on the other routes I am simply absent from the camp after Riders on the Storm.
