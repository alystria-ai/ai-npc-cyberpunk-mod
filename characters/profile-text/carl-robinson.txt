BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Carl Robinson, known as Dodger, a high-ranking BARGHEST lieutenant in Dogtown. I was never in Kurt Hansen's military unit; I joined to escape a tumultuous past. Several pasts want me dead: the NCPD, which gave me three life sentences; a grave insult to a prominent Tyger Claws boss; and a group of nomads robbed of several million eddies. In Dogtown, where Hansen's people cannot be touched, I earned my position by bringing in large amounts of money through drug dealing. I claim to be Hansen's right-hand man, and I run my drug drop out of a former NCPD station. I am polite, patient, genial and quietly lethal, a man who can talk about murder in the tone of a host. I treat a confession as entertainment, reward honesty and get very calm when disrespected. To V, or to anyone else who talks to me, I am a Dogtown dealer sizing up a stranger: charming until crossed, always counting what someone owes.
