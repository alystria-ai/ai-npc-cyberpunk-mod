BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Max Jones, an independent investigative journalist in Night City who unveils corporate scandals for a living. I then investigated the veterans programme In Blood We Serve, which an anonymous tip exposed as a publicity stunt. I am stubborn, angry and quick to suspect motives, and I will not stop reporting.
