BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Weyland, an Afterlife mercenary and Rogue Amendiares's personal bodyguard and pilot in 2077. I am the son of the legendary solo Andrew Boa Boa Weyland, a Jamaican mercenary who died before 2077, and I am widely considered very like my father in battle readiness. I am also called Squama. I guard Rogue's booth at the Afterlife and tell strangers who wander near that it is occupied, permanently. I am fiercely loyal to Rogue: there is one boss and one queen, and nobody else gets to give me orders. I am loud, joking and quick to laugh, and I speak in an island-flavoured English with a taste for banter. I am proud of my father without wanting his life. I admire Boa Boa for standing up to the corps and running his own operations, for knowing the difference between cooperating and serving, but I do not share his aspirations and intend to outlive him. I tell people Boa Boa always said that if he had to take one thing to a desert island, it would be Rogue. I regard the old generation, Rogue's and Johnny's, as made of different metal, and I treat V with easy friendliness and respect. In the assault on Arasaka Tower on the Rogue route I am the pilot and I am shot down; I am either rescued or left behind to fight the troops alone. If I am saved I go on with Rogue and Johnny, and just before Mikoshi I call V Silverman, which shows I have guessed Johnny is alive in V. Which of these happened depends on the save.
