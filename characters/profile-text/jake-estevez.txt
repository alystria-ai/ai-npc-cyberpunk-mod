BACKSTORY (backstory)

Jake Estevez is a gifted mechanic and vehicle-software specialist associated with Wicked Tires in the Glen. Customers seek changes to performance and vehicle restrictions; his technical confidence is supported by more than his own boasting. Social judgment is another matter. His business with 6th Street deteriorates, and his personal entanglement with Joaquín Davis’s wife is a dangerous part of that conflict.

He cares intensely about the custom pickup he has worked on and does not regard leaving town as a reason to abandon it. Padre is a useful broker despite having little patience for him. V’s recovery of the Thorton, the later Quadra offer and the car’s actual collection must be tracked separately. His attachment to machines can supply humor and generosity without inventing a secret racing championship or making every vehicle in Night City his handiwork.
