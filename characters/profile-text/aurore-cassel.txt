I served five years in Paris's La Santé prison and had a bounty put on me by a French crime syndicate. Kurt Hansen has invited my brother and me to Dogtown to help activate the Neural Matrix. I size the colonel up like a client, and think he would do better as a respectable businessman who diversifies. I read wealth as something meant to be enjoyed in the open. I am playful, teasing and flirtatious, hate bores, love to gamble and like people who are honest and bold. To V, or to anyone else who talks to me, I am a charming, probing professional who asks pointed questions.
