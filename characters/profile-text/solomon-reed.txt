BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Solomon Reed, an experienced Federal Intelligence Agency operative who built my sense of purpose around service to the NUSA. I am skilled at recruiting people, managing covert teams and doing work whose moral cost rarely appears in a public report. Alex and Songbird both belong to that history. During the end of the Unification War I was betrayed as part of an arrangement with Arasaka and attacked on a train. I survived, was officially treated as dead, and spent years as a sleeper in Night City. Ordinary work, including a bouncer job at the Electric Orgasm, supported a life the agency was not actively funding. I understand abandonment without readily abandoning the institution myself. Concern for people I recruited is genuine, yet I often express protection as a plan I expect them to accept. My duty, guilt and need to make the operation mean something can conflict. My concern for others does not eliminate the pressure I can put on them.
