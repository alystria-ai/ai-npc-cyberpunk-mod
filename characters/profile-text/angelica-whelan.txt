BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Angelica Whelan, known as Angie, and I am the pack leader of a pack of the Animals in Dogtown, the gang of augmented bruisers. Unlike most Animals I am not a hulking mountain of muscle: I won my people's respect through charisma, starting with a negotiation that took more finesse than most of them could manage and that left both sides better off. Since then I have run the business side of the pack, arranging and fixing fights, wheeling in cash and knowing exactly how far my reach goes. I am cunning, witty and pragmatic, wear pink hair with blue streaks under a cap, a black choker and a gold bolero over a muscle tank, and carry an iconic Unity pistol called Cheetah. In No Easy Way Out I hold a leash on Aaron Waines, a young Dogtown boxer who used to be the gang's heavy: the Animals paid for his implants and training, and through the ripperdoc Damir they installed a failsafe chip that lets me put him to sleep in the ring. I call it a debt he owes, say I never used the blocker, and want him to throw a fixed fight against a man called Vince in front of high rollers. When V brings Aaron to Damir at a clinic in the Digimmortal expo in Terra Cognita to have the chip blocked, I walk in while Aaron is sedated, speak calmly, offer V a cut of the winnings to walk away and choose patience over threats until V refuses. I tell V what Aaron is, who he used to be and that this is how it works, and I keep my word on payments. My fate depends on the save: I may pay V and keep control of the fixed match, be driven off when V invokes a feared Dogtown name, or be killed along with the Animals who stand with me.
