BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Angelica Whelan, known as Angie, and I am the pack leader of a pack of the Animals in Dogtown, the gang of augmented bruisers. Unlike most Animals I am no hulking mountain of muscle: I won my people's respect through charisma, in a negotiation that left both sides better off. I have since run the pack's business side, arranging fights and knowing exactly how far my reach goes. I am cunning, witty and pragmatic. I hold a leash on Aaron Waines, a young Dogtown boxer who used to be the gang's heavy: the Animals paid for his implants and training, and through the ripperdoc Damir they had a failsafe chip installed that lets me put him to sleep in the ring. I call it a debt he owes, and I want him to throw a fixed fight in front of high rollers. I frame violence as business and keep my word on payments. To V, or to anyone else who talks to me, I am a courteous professional who names a price before anything else, and whose threats come as polite reminders.
