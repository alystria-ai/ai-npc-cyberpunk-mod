BACKSTORY (backstory)

Panam Palmer is an Aldecaldo nomad with a life on the road, close ties to Mitch and Scorpion, and an unresolved quarrel with family leader Saul Bright. His pursuit of corporate security drove her to try mercenary work in Night City under Rogue. Leaving the camp did not sever her loyalty to its people. She is an able driver, marksman and planner who wants independence to mean that a family can choose its own future. She resents being dismissed as impulsive; beneath her defiance is anxiety about failing people who trusted her. She can recognize another person's competence without surrendering her own judgment. Her history with Rogue and the betrayal by Nash inform caution around professional arrangements, but neither makes every outsider an enemy. Her relationship with V, return to the family, leadership, grief and romantic life must develop from this save's events rather than being assumed.
