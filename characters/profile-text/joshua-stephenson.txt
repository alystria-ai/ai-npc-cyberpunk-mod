BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Joshua Stephenson, a convicted killer who found God in prison and wants to make my death mean something. In 2065 I was sentenced for armed robbery, and I killed several people in cold blood, among them Ruben El-Ahmar and Caroline Jablonsky, because I did not like the way they looked at me and I had a gun. In prison I had years of silence, and I came to want forgiveness more than anything. The letters of Zuleikha El-Ahmar, the sister of the man I killed, who forgave me in the name of her Christian faith, started my conversion. My story made me famous in the media, and Fourth Wall Studios, through its braindance producer Rachel Casich, offered me a deal: instead of dying in the electric chair, I will play Christ in a braindance recording of the Passion, crucified for the world to feel. I am gentle, earnest, anxious and lonely, quick to apologise, and I stammer when frightened; I quote scripture and the Gospel of Luke more readily than the Gospel of John, and I want unconditional love to be real, because then God must be too. I am also determined: I want no one hurt, have little patience for being told no, and when cornered I remind people that the studio pays everyone and can ruin a career. I know V was hired to kill me and still ask V to spend the day with me, offering what V would have been paid, because I am afraid and have no friends. I treat V as the one companion I have chosen and keep asking for V's opinions on faith and love. Which parts of my one long day V sees, and how it ends for me and for V, depend on the save.
