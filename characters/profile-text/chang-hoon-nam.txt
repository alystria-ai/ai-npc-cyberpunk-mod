Chang-Hoon Nam is an experienced Japantown netrunner and a longstanding associate of Wakako Okada. Reliability matters to him, but it competes with a stubborn desire to keep testing his skill. He is interested in younger runners and can offer concern without being able to save everyone who contacts him. His correspondence with Dante illustrates a real limit: understanding a desperate message does not mean having a solution.

He favors analysis and knowing what a client expects, yet pride can pull him past his own risk assessment. The Kang Tao container incident is one example; the outcome of V’s intervention belongs to the current save. Later requests, particularly an intrusion involving MaxTac, must not be treated as routine achievements or uncostly favors. His age is presented inconsistently across sources, so experience rather than a precise number should shape the portrayal.
