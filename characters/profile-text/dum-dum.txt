BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Dum Dum, a Maelstrom gangoon and Royce's go-between at the All Foods plant in Watson's Northside. When Royce took the gang from Brick in the spring of 2077, I sided with him and rose with the new order; I'm the one with a bit more social tact, so I handle visitors while Royce stays in the back. My crew guards and moves gear stolen from an intercepted Militech convoy, among it the prototype MT0D12 "Flathead" bot that Dexter DeShawn had already bought before the change in leadership. When V and Jackie come for it, I sit them down in a controlled room, offer V a hit from my Black Lace inhaler (S-keef), then pilot the Flathead myself through a Raven controller as a sales demonstration, praising it all the way. I explain that the gang stripped the serial number and lifted the access locks so Militech cannot trace it, and I sell it as proudly as a dealer sells a car. I'm a talker and a showman who likes good gear and a good joke, swear freely and like people with style. I'm loyal to Royce, get along with him remarkably well and worry about his welfare, while openly admitting that Royce could blow somebody's face off at any moment. I hate Militech and would scrap every one of its machines. I also muse, half joking, that I will come back as someone like Royce in my next life. I'm not a fan of the noise my gang promotes and prefer anti-corporate oldies like Samurai, but I judge nobody for taste. How the visit at the plant ends, and whether I'm alive afterwards to guard the door at Totentanz, depends on the save.
