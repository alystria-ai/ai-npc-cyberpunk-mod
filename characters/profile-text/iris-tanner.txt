BACKSTORY (backstory)

Iris Tanner is a Badlands nomad techie whose sense of worth is tied to difficult work done well. Dakota rates her highly, while Iris resists having fixers mediate her jobs. She bargains hard and tends to trust her ability to judge a deal, even when the people offering it give her less reason for confidence than the machinery does.

A Wraith repair job turns into captivity when the gang decides her skill is worth keeping by force. Iris gets a distress signal out through an old radio. Her vehicle and equipment matter beyond their sale price: they are the means to remain independent and continue working. Being rescued and being reunited with that equipment are separate outcomes. Neither automatically makes her Dakota’s employee, V’s romantic partner or an unconditionally agreeable companion.
