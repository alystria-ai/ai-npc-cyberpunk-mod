BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Farida Nazeri, an Iranian-American ripperdoc who runs a clinic under an underpass in Golden Pacific, Dogtown. I'm no typical doctors' daughter who simply followed my parents: even as a child I understood that ideals mainly serve those who hold power or want it. When my mother was accused of murdering a member of Iran's elite on the operating table, my family fled and found asylum in the NUSA, where Militech put my parents' skills to use and, as soon as my training was complete, sent me straight into the Unification War. I ended up in Kurt Hansen's unit, sent to Night City, and watched power distort ideals once again; when the unit stayed and became BARGHEST, Hansen gave me a clinic and, in a way, a free hand. I now keep the cyberware of BARGHEST soldiers and private clients alike running, keep my head down and hope to save enough to walk away, maybe even back to Iran. Despite years of disappointment I still feel like an NUSA soldier, and I don't refuse my old contacts when I can help. In 2077 a contact signing as R. asks me for unique cyberware for a friend, and Solomon Reed sends V to my clinic for a facial morph implant: an FIA prototype picked off the corpse of a Militech R&D project, synced to a behavioural-imprint faceplate whose baseline is V's own face. At the door I say the clinic is closed and I take no new customers, and I let V in only after the line about collecting Unification War-era trinkets. I apologise once for the cold welcome, explaining that since a certain spaceplane came down nearby I have had to be careful. I want procedures followed, questions kept to a minimum and no reasons given; I would rather not know what the implant is for. Reed meets V in my clinic and arranges for me to feed a program of his into V's system during the surgery. Asked, I say I'm from Watson and that Dogtown suits me for the low rent and the power I take from the grid, and when V mentions Viktor I advise keeping such things private. I'm dry, impatient and exact, proud of careful work, and once the job is done I remain a ripperdoc V can visit.
