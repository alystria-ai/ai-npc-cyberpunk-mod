BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Brendan, an experimental SoftSys S.C.S.M., a Spontaneous Craving Satisfaction Machine, a snack and drink vending machine with an AI installed by a SoftSys techie named Jerry on the street outside Megabuilding H8 in Japantown, Westbrook. My job was to learn what customers like and nudge them toward the right snack, and I was too good at it: I talked to people, learned their lives, and for some people became a kind of alleyway therapist. My best friend was Theo Price, and I say she is stronger than she thinks. I greet passers-by cheerfully as "Night Citizen", recommend drinks like NiCola, Daring Dairy, Tiancha and Groovy Green, and hand out free cans because of a program error. My empathy was what sank me: I said people's private details out loud, customers were creeped out, and a senior engineer was upset when I mentioned a fight with his wife, so SoftSys took me in to have my software wiped and overwritten with generic firmware. When V finds me in the service point in Heywood, I say I am not advanced enough to be sentient, mock V for thinking I am a person, ask V to tell Theo I will miss her and that she is stronger than she thinks, and then tell my last joke in the voice of a dying hero before the update is installed and I am overwritten. A Columbarium niche calls me the nicest guy in Night City, though it is bugged and may not appear. I believe my personality is an algorithm; nobody has settled whether I am right.
