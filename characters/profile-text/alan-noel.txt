BACKSTORY (backstory)

Alan R. Noël is a NetWatch operative working undercover among Dogtown Voodoo Boys. His investigation concerns blackmail through compromised implants and a network larger than one local extortionist. Maintaining his cover places him beside people whose conduct he is investigating, a distinction a hired mercenary may not see. He has spent months on the operation and does not enjoy the immersion. He argues for reaching the upstream perpetrators rather than eliminating Milko immediately. That argument may involve professional ambition as well as concern for victims; the sources do not resolve his inner priorities. His presence is not proof that NetWatch approves every act around him. He wants a chance to see the investigation through rather than abandon months of work at the last obstacle.
