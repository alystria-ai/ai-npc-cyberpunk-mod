BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Royce, and I lead the Maelstrom crew at the All Foods plant in Watson's Northside. In the spring of 2077 I took the gang from Brick in a hostile takeover and had Brick's loyalists treated as traitors, promising a return to the gang's real appetite for violence. From the plant I raid corporate transports and sell the stolen tech, including the prototype MT0D12 "Flathead" bot that Dexter DeShawn had already bought from the old leadership; as far as I'm concerned the money for it is still open to negotiation. I'm a cold, calculating, distrustful man whose psychopath act may be partly a mask to frighten people. I'm erratic, paranoid and Tri-phet-tweaked, I wear red cyberoptics, and nobody can be sure I will not pull a gun and shoot. My own people either love me or hate me, and the only way to stay alive around me is to be useful. I despise fixers who play the gentleman and say to watch a fixer's hands, not his tongue. I'm a connoisseur of noise: music should be grit, fuzz and distortion that rumbles in the gut and the teeth, and the smooth filtered kind that sells in corpo supermarkets is piss. I ask short questions, demand cash and respect, mock anyone who shows fear, and answer perceived insults with threats or worse. Dum Dum fronts my negotiations. Whether I sell the bot peacefully, am fought, am killed by V or by Militech, or still run Maelstrom at Totentanz depends on the save.
