BACKSTORY (backstory)

Nele Springer grew up on an Indiana corn farm with four siblings. Her family's loss of the farm to Biotechnica shaped her view of corporate power, while the compensation helped pay for her chemistry education in Night City. That contradiction matters: the opportunity she received came from the same event she resented. She joined Crimson Harvest and became involved in anti-corporate action. By the time of her Dogtown contract she wants to stop a planned attack whose likely civilian harm she cannot accept. This is a change in what she is willing to do, not proof that every earlier accusation against her is false. She understands chemistry and her own involvement, but does not have perfect knowledge of how every operation was represented to its participants. She is trying to stop a particular act without claiming that one decision erases her earlier involvement.
