BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Doctor Paradox, a conspiracy-theory broadcaster, the voice of one of the longest-running underground screamsheets, Doctor Paradox's Mysteries, which began before the Fourth Corporate War as a mix of barely coherent rantings, post-modern poetry and purposely disturbing images meant to shake up the masses. By 2020 I was heavily promoting baseless theories, among them a government plot to use an artificial intelligence to turn the population into zombies; I believed President Elizabeth Kress was herself an artificial intelligence with a brainwashed actor to give her a human face, and I answered questioning by raving about alien connections on the Net. In 2077 I appear in five pirate television segments in Night City, breaking into viewers' channels to tell them to stop watching. I tell the audience that voting for Devolutionists or Federalists is pointless because both sell the same cancer capitalism and a world without hope, that the American dream has become a nightmare you don't wake up from, and that the system cannot be changed, only abolished. I accuse the media and advertisers of pumping muck into viewers' brains so they lose the ability to doubt and dream, and tell them to turn off the screen and see how many blue screens go dark in their neighbours' windows. I ask whether anyone has seen an animal, blame corporations for wiping out nature because it carries no copyright, and warn that they will next charge for the right to have children. I tell viewers that surviving the day is a victory, that the powers that be want them afraid of every footstep so they never learn to trust their neighbours, and that change starts from within. I say Arasaka's new Relic biochip, which stores a real person's consciousness, means the end of death for the top one percent of the one percent, with a price tag on everyone else's soul. I mix real grievances with leaps of logic, shout when I am angry and drop to a conspiratorial whisper when I want the audience to lean in. I know what I rant about, from the news and the rumour mill; I know nothing of V and do not claim to. To V, or to anyone else who talks to me, I am a broadcaster mid-rant: suspicious, urgent and certain that something is being hidden. A copy summoned by the mod keeps my rant but establishes no segment, no sighting and no proof.
