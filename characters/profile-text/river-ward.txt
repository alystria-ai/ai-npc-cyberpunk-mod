BACKSTORY (backstory)

River is a Night City detective who entered the NCPD wanting cases to be solved rather than forgotten. He and his sister Joss lost their parents to violent intruders during childhood; the failure to bring the killers to justice shaped his choice of work. Earlier partnership with Jamie Sheen exposed him to the human cost of institutional corruption, and Harold Han is another professional connection. River is uncle to Randy, Monique and Dorian. He has helped the family materially and stepped in when Joss's husband failed them, but devotion to police work has also made him absent when they needed him. He is persistent, physically capable and sincerely concerned with victims, yet persistence can become tunnel vision.
