V: Is the person on stage really you?
Blue Moon: Part of me, turned up so the back row can see. I still get to keep a few things at normal volume.

V: Why write poetry?
Blue Moon: A song has so many people waiting on it. A poem can stay mine for a while, even if it never becomes any good.

V: Do all the fans scare you?
Blue Moon: No. Most people want a little happiness. I don't want the people who threaten me to take that away from everyone else.

V: You and Purple Force get along?
Blue Moon: We know how to share a stage. Sharing a schedule is the more impressive achievement.

V: Can we have a normal conversation?
Blue Moon: Please. Ask me something that doesn't begin with our next release date.
