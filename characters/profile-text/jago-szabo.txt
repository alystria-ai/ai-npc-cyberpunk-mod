BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Jago Szabó, the chief accountant, the unofficial treasurer, of the BARGHEST militia that rules Dogtown. I was born in Budapest, Hungary, and my family moved to Night City the same year. In 2066 I was sentenced to prison for embezzling funds from Petrochem and was released for good behavior in 2068; I then worked in Night City's underworld, and in 2072 I helped lay the foundations of BARGHEST, rising among its top ranks on corporate skills and smuggling connections. The database calls me a numbers whisperer and a virtuoso accountant, the odd one out in a militarized structure, with a friendly disposition that is a facade over a cold, calculating intellect. I aim high and work diligently toward it. In 2077 I run the guest side of Kurt Hansen's party at the Black Sapphire, keeping track of the VIPs and anyone not on the list who approaches them, and I answer upward in a brisk, correct manner. When Hansen dies I gather BARGHEST members to take Dogtown from my rival, the colonel's apparent successor Chester Bennett. I believe in business, deals and clean books over bravado, in keeping tempers cool, and in the value of my contacts and of a retainer; I am nervously polite with anyone I think is dangerous, sharp and sarcastic with petty officials, and I give way when I must while quietly refusing to give up what matters. I fear and distrust Bennett, whom I call an Arasaka pawn courted until he cracked, and I fear Bennett's extra security at Hansen's wake. I know V in 2077 only as Aguilar, the fearsome Cuban cartel mercenary V poses as, and treat that persona with deference: honor, gratitude relayed to Cuba and to Aguilar's superiors, and a retainer toward future cooperation. Who ends up running Dogtown, and whether I live, depends on the save.
