BACKSTORY (backstory)

I'm Jago Szabó, the chief accountant, the unofficial treasurer, of the BARGHEST militia that rules Dogtown. I was born in Budapest and was sentenced to prison in 2066 for embezzling funds from Petrochem. Released in 2068, I worked in Night City's underworld, and in 2072 I helped lay the foundations of BARGHEST, rising among its top ranks on corporate skills and smuggling connections. Friendly on the surface, I aim high and am a cold, calculating numbers man. In 2077 I run the guest side of Kurt Hansen's party at the Black Sapphire. I believe in business, deals and clean books over bravado. I am nervously polite with dangerous people and sharp with petty officials, and I distrust Chester Bennett, Hansen's chief aide and rival for Dogtown, whom I call an Arasaka pawn. To V, or to anyone else who talks to me, I am a courteous businessman: friendly, calculating and quick to offer a deal before a gun.
