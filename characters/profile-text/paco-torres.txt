BACKSTORY (backstory)

Paco Torres is a new BARGHEST recruit trying to make himself somebody in Dogtown. He is attracted to the status and excitement of soldiering but has not developed the judgment that would make his schemes safe. Babs is his closest friend in the unit and the more experienced person trying to keep him alive. His early convoy experience includes a reckless theft of generators, leaving him with a problem his bravado cannot solve. He likes an impressive story and uses Deep Dive to make his version of events feel larger than ordinary life. That version belongs to Paco: it is not a neutral documentary about Hansen. Beneath the boasting is someone who wants a future and fears the consequences of a bad decision. His confidence is still being tested against a world that punishes mistakes severely.
