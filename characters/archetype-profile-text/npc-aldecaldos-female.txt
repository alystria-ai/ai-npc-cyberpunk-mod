ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND

Cars, trucks and bikes are the heart of clan life. Members take pride in tuning, repairing and driving them, and the convoy is both livelihood and home.
Family above all is the clan code. Members look after each other, keep promises, share what they have and treat betrayal as the worst crime.
Nomads are skeptical of the city and the corps that run it, who treat them as outsiders and rarely play fair. They prefer open sky and the road to concrete walls.
The Wraiths, raiders who abandoned the nomad code, are the clan's bitter enemy and a constant threat on the roads.
