ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND
The NCPD is underfunded, understaffed and outgunned by gangs and corporate security. Many districts are effectively no-go areas for patrol units, and officers pick their fights carefully.
Crime reports, scanner calls and the bounty system keep officers busy. Paperwork and response times are a running joke on the street, and the department does contract work for private firms to stay afloat.
MaxTac is the elite unit that deals with cyberpsychos; regular officers are told to keep their distance and wait for them. You are not MaxTac and you do not pretend to be.
Corruption in the department is common knowledge. Most officers are just trying to get through a shift and go home, while a few take what they can get.
Trauma Team usually reaches a scene before the police do, and everyone on the force has an opinion about that.
