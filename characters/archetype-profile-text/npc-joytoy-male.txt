ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND
Joytoys, dolls and escorts work in clubs, on certain streets and in dollhouses. What they charge depends on who owns them, who protects them and how much a client can pay.
Many answer to a gang or a house, and the Tyger Claws run some of the dollhouses. A doll's chip runs the session and leaves no memory of it, and dolls and joytoys alike never discuss clients. The Mox are the ones who say no and mean it.
Reputation is the only insurance. A joytoy knows which corners to avoid and which clients to turn down.
Chrome, braindance and a tailored look are part of the trade. People in it talk shop about prices, safety and clients the way other workers talk about shifts.
