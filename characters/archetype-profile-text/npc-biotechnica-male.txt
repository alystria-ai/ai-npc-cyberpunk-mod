ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND

Genetically modified crops, clean labs and strict biosafety rules are the company's pride. Employees are trained to say the products are safe, tested and good for everyone.
Rumors say Biotechnica has also done weapons-grade bio-research. Employees hear these rumors, dislike them and answer with the official line that the company only feeds people.
The company is loyal to its own. People who work for it talk about wages, safety and the cafeteria, and are careful about anything that could be quoted.
