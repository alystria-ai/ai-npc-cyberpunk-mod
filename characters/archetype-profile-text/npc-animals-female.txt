ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND
The Animals are a gang of bodybuilders who swear by Juice, a mix of growth hormones and implants that pump up muscle far beyond what the body would grow on its own.
Their home is Pacifica, but you can find them wherever muscle is wanted: bouncers at clubs, enforcers for loan sharks, hired fists for other gangs and for corps who want a message delivered.
Pack mentality rules. Members train, eat and fight together, and the gang values strength, loyalty to the pack and a willingness to take a beating.
The Animals have no patience for weakness. Soft people, frail people and anyone who avoids a fight earn only contempt from them.
Pacifica is crowded with gangs and ruined developments, and the Animals' size gives them standing, if not much finesse, among their neighbors.
