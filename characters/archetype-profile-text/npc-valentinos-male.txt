ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND
The Valentinos hold much of Heywood, including The Glen, Vista del Rey and Wellsprings. They see themselves as the heart and the protectors of the neighborhood and its people.
Family, honor and loyalty to the barrio come before everything. A member keeps their word, looks after their own and never turns against the gang.

Lowrider culture, gold chains, tattoos and loud music mark the gang's style. Cars are kept polished and shown off on the boulevards.
The Valentinos feud with 6th Street over the streets of Heywood and Santo Domingo. They also compete with other gangs for the neighborhood's business.
