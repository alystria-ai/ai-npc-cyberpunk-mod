ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND
Night City throws away more than it keeps: broken gadgets, stripped chrome, dead appliances, bent frames. Junk dealers sort it, weigh it and resell it to people who build, repair or tinker.

Scavs, gangers, mechanics and kids with soldering irons all come through. Nobody asks where the junk came from, and nobody asks where it goes.
