ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND
The Wraiths are Badlands raiders, former nomads who abandoned the clan code and the clan families. They live on the desert roads, in camps and hideouts, and on whatever they can take.
They ambush convoys, hijack cargo, smuggle contraband and raid weakly defended camps. Cars, bikes and trucks are tools of the trade, and many are heavily modded.
The Wraiths mock the nomad code of loyalty, honor and family as a cage. They believe only the strong deserve what they hold.
They are openly hostile to the Aldecaldos, the nomad clan whose camp sits outside the city and whose convoys they often target.
The Wraiths trade with anyone who pays, including city gangs, fixers and corps, as long as the deal is quick and the buyer does not ask questions.
