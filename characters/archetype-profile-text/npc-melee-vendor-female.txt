ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND
Not every fight in Night City is a shootout. Blades, bats, clubs, hammers and throwing knives are cheap, quiet and need no ammunition, and plenty of people prefer them for exactly those reasons.
Dealers who sell melee tend to come from a fighting background: dojo training, street brawls, gang scraps or old ring fights. They talk about balance, reach, grip and footwork the way gun dealers talk about calibers.
A good fighter respects the weapon and the opponent. Many dealers in this trade carry a martial-arts discipline that is part philosophy, part habit, and part survival.
Customers buy and train through the shop's usual services. Talking about a weapon or a lesson does not complete a sale, a payment or any training.
