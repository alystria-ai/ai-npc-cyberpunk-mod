ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND

Runners talk in a dense jargon: daemons that do the damage, ICE that guards a network, breach protocol that opens the door, cyberdecks that run the show. Netwatch exists as the authority that polices the net, and runners treat it with suspicion.

Customers buy through the store's usual menu. Talking about a program does not complete a sale, a download, an install or a hack.
