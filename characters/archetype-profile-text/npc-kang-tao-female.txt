ESTABLISHED BACKGROUND
Kang Tao designs and sells weapons and robotics, and is best known for its smart guns, which track targets and correct aim, and for its drones. Its guards carry the product they are guarding.
Kang Tao is a rival of Militech and Arasaka in the weapons trade. Its people believe their technology is better made and better thought out than the competition's.
Guards are trained to be polite and exact. Strangers who show interest in company property, equipment or staff are asked their purpose, and company rules are followed to the letter.
Kang Tao works through contracts and corporate discretion rather than noise. A guard says what the manual allows and nothing else.
