# Mimi decoder v2 (phase-anchored), October 6, 2026

Decoder-only fine-tune of Pocket's Mimi decoder (policy `mimi_decoder_gan_v1`, trainer `scripts/pocket_training/mimi_decoder.py`). It replaces decoder A, which sounded "holographic" on some voices (first noticed on Carol Emeka).

## What caused the holographic sound
- Only decoder A has it; the pretrained decoder is normal, and the faint tint left on Carol line 0 ("well") is in the pretrained decoder too.
- It is not the training data and not the highs. Listening controls (same latents, one cause removed at a time) showed: removing the 200 Hz line comb, the highs, tails or brightness changes nothing; putting the pretrained **phase** back into decoder A fixes it. Phase in 1.5-4 kHz fixes most; 0.5-4 kHz fixes Carol completely.
- Decoder A's phase differs from the pretrained decoder's (phase agreement 0.6-0.8 in 1.5-4 kHz; sounds normal at 0.94 or more). The mel loss constrains magnitude only, and the GAN left the phase free.
- Decoder A also adds a comb of persistent lines at multiples of 200 Hz (its upsampler's frame grid); inaudible alone, but penalised in v2.
- No per-character effects are wanted; the effect-heavy clips are excluded from training only because they are bad data.

## Recipe (decoder v2)
- Same as A (mel 15, multi-scale STFT GAN, feature matching, EMA, lr 2e-5), plus:
  - **phase anchor** (`--lambda-anchor 20`): magnitude-weighted phase distance to the frozen pretrained decoder on the same latents, 0.5-4 kHz only, magnitude left free;
  - 200 Hz line penalty and a fine long-term-spectrum match (`--lambda-grid 2`, `--lambda-ltas 20`);
  - clean data: 7,652 original clips + 14,112 expanded-pool clips, with the exclusion list (`scripts/phone_detect/effect-exclusions.json`: 411 training clips and 1,605 pool clips; band-limited, comb/flanger, persistent tone, long reverb; metadata-only phone/holocall clips are kept);
  - gate: held-out phase coherence with the pretrained decoder (worse of 0.5-1.5 and 1.5-4 kHz) at least 0.92, for all lines and for Carol separately.
- Run: 7,000 steps with a complex-L1 anchor weight 10 (run `v2`, stopped when a pool file was deleted mid-run), then 9,590 steps from that EMA with the phase-only anchor (run `v2b`, discriminator restarted). About 16.5k steps in total on the 4080, Balanced profile.
- Held-out (80 lines): log-mel 1.40 (pretrained) -> 1.00 (A 0.84), error above 8 kHz 6.5 -> 1.85 dB (A 2.08), phase coherence 0.990, Carol 0.988, 200 Hz line excess -1.4 dB (A +2.8).

Reading: v2 keeps the texture gain (PLS within 1-3 of A) and removes the artifact signature, but it gives back about half of A's ECAPA gain (+0.004 to +0.010 over the pretrained decoder, against +0.019 to +0.023 for A; about half the lines' standard error, so treat the gap as indicative). The phase gate sits at 0.99, far above the 0.92 floor, so there is room to loosen the anchor (weight 5-10) and recover some ECAPA; whether it stays free of the artifact is a listening question.
