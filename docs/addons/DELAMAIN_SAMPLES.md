The best lines from the eval rides, with the moment and facts each answered, so the persona can be judged before shipping. All were
generated by Mistral `ministral-8b-latest` (the add-on's default route) from the persona in `addons/delamain/convai-addon.json` (then in `persona.json`), through
`node scripts/addon-eval.mjs` and the eval rides in `addons/delamain/eval/`. Nothing here was edited. Lines that only copied a
persona example (for example "Corpo Plaza. Do try not to sign anything.") are left out, because they show the examples, not the
model.

Scores and the remaining weaknesses are at the end. The character rules are in `docs/addons/DELAMAIN_BIBLE.md`.

## Scores

Two judges. The eval judge (`addon-eval --judge`, Mistral `ministral-14b`) is lenient: almost every line gets 8 or 9 whatever
changes. The blind strict judge (Claude Haiku, 30 lines sampled across the rides, a rubric of five criteria x 2 points plus flags)
is the one that tells versions apart.

| Version | Lines | Eval judge | Strict judge | Dropped repeats | Talk answers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Writer's baseline (persona file, 3 runs) | 209 | 8.67 | **6.6** | 10 of 237 | 25 to 50 words |
| Same code as the baseline, my conditions (9 of 10 rides; `short-hops` was rate limited) | 73 | 8.72 | **6.4** | 2 of 73 | avg 30 words, 26 of 33 over 25 |
| Quality pass, first run | 79 | 8.61 | **7.0** | 0 of 79 (4 regenerated) | avg 18.4 words, 3 of 34 over 25 |
| Quality pass, final run | 76 | 8.63 | **7.9** | 3 of 79 (3 regenerated) | avg 17.5 words, 2 of 34 over 25 |

Final strict run, by flag: invented detail 7 (a rain memory with no weather fact, a dry back seat, an Aldecaldos anecdote, a
five-minute saving, a district, a fare), quest outcome implied 2, too long 1, ride control 0, broke character 0, insensitive 0.
The bar in `docs/ADDON_API.md` (average 7 or more with no dropped repeats) is met on the average; three remarks were still dropped
after one regeneration (one was a word-for-word repeat of an earlier arrival line, which is the check working).

A middle run went back to a lenient 8.71 but the lines grew new tics: naming a phrase in a rule, or putting it in a sample, made the
8b model use it ("The destination...", "I shall make it two"). The fix was positive "in the spirit of" scripts and a rule that says
"fresh words" without quoting any phrase. It applies to any persona.

## Weaknesses left

- **Invented detail** is the main strict deduction (7 of 30 lines): rain, a dry seat or a clan anecdote when the ride block does not
  give them, and now and then a number. The ride block says "use only", but an 8b model still decorates.
- **Quest outcome**: the guard blocks for the rogue-cab and first-night questions cut it a lot, but one line still said the fragments
  were "contained", and one said "I have always been" to Panam. Nothing verified says what each `sq025_delamain_fate` value means,
  so he stays ambiguous and the bridge and the Lua never read it (a test pins that).
- **Stock words**: "optimal", "precision" and "modest fare" recur across rides (the repeat check ignores single words by design).
- **A talk answer that restates a remark** (`short-hops`: 35 words, the arrival line repeated). One in 34 talk answers.
- **Ordinary wit**: about 12 of 30 lines are pleasant rather than gems; the best are in the table above.
- **Fragmented mode** still reads as trailing dots and moody images more than distinct former personalities.
