# Delamain: sample lines (2026-10-13)

The best lines from the eval rides, with the moment and facts each answered, so the persona can be judged before shipping. All were
generated by Mistral `ministral-8b-latest` (the add-on's default route) from `addons/delamain/persona.json`, through
`node scripts/addon-eval.mjs` and the eval rides in `addons/delamain/eval/`. Nothing here was edited. Lines that only copied a
persona example (for example "Corpo Plaza. Do try not to sign anything.") are left out, because they show the examples, not the
model.

Scores and the remaining weaknesses are at the end. The character rules are in `docs/addons/DELAMAIN_BIBLE.md`.

## Weaknesses left

- **Talk replies are too long.** Remarks are tight (10 to 20 words); answers to V run 25 to 50 words and drift into the weather or
  recite the distance and fare. The main mod's output rules allow 15 to 40 words and outrank the persona. This is the strict judge's
  most frequent deduction.
- **Invented details**: an umbrella, murals, "air conditioning", rain that "has stopped" (or begins with no weather fact), and
  memories of the first night beyond what the persona gives.
- **The quest outcome**: asked which Delamain he is, or what became of the fragments, the 8b model sometimes says "the same one",
  "the original", or "reassigned", despite the rule and the example. Giving him the real outcome (a mode chosen from
  `sq025_delamain_fate`) would be more robust than asking a small model to stay ambiguous.
- **Companions**: Panam is sometimes "he"; the companion is sometimes pulled into an answer meant for V.
- **The first night with Jackie**: usually gentle, but some runs add invented, slightly lurid details.
- **Fragmented mode** reads as trailing dots and moody images more than distinct former personalities.
- **Example copying**: a few example punchlines come back word for word ("Do try not to sign anything", "The fare, alas, is not so
  easily left behind"). Fine once; at the new pacing they will recur across rides.
