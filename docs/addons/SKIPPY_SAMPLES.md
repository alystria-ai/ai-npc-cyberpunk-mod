`addons/skippy/eval/` through `scripts/addon-eval.mjs`, using Mistral with the user's key. Each line is shown exactly as the
model spoke it. The Model column shows which speaker model wrote it: "8B" is ministral-8b, the API's first choice, and "14B" is
ministral-14b. The character bible is `docs/addons/SKIPPY_BIBLE.md`.

## Quality numbers

All runs used Mistral. The built-in judge is ministral-14b with the API's `JUDGE_RUBRIC`.

- "Builder bridge" is the builder's `addon-skippy` bridge, unchanged.
- "Device bridge" is that bridge with the changes in section 11 of the bible: rotated devices, distance only at 30 m or more,
  no raw keys, and opener tracking.
- "Asterisk fix" simulates keeping the word inside single-word `*emphasis*`. Today `cleanReply` deletes it.

| Run | Setup | Lines | Built-in judge | Dropped repeats | Sample echoes | Over word limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1 | first persona, builder bridge, as is | 74 | 8.41 | 6 | 16 | n/a |
| v5 as is | builder bridge, no fix | 115 | 8.20 | 13 | 10 | n/a |
| v5 recommended | device bridge + asterisk fix | 122 | 8.20 | 6 | 6 | n/a |
| v6 recommended | final persona, device bridge + fix, 8B | 110 | 8.24 | 10 | 3 | 24 |
| 14B | same setup, ministral-14b, 4 fights (02, 03, 05, 12) | 59 | 8.46 | 2 | 3 | 34 |

The v1 to v6 runs covered all 12 test fights, one run each. In the as-is setup, 41 of 128 raw replies contained asterisks. With
the final persona, 19 of 122 still did, and each of those loses a word today.

**Second, blind judge (Haiku, the same five-point rubric, harsher scale where 5 is filler and 7 is genuinely good):**

The blind judge sees no difference in wit between 14B and 8B. 14B's gains are elsewhere: fewer broken lines, fewer wrong facts

The two judges disagree on the level, not the order of the best lines. The built-in judge gives almost everything 8 or 9. The
blind judge rates most lines as filler and picks out the same handful of strong ones.

## What still goes wrong

- **Over the word limit:** about a third of the lines.
- **Cross-fight motifs:** these come back from fight to fight ("need a cane soon", "bum bum be-dum" in prepared lines, warranty
  clauses). The 8-line repeat window cannot see them.
- **Wrong numbers:** distances rounded wrongly, or "Seven more" when the count went from 4 to 7.
- **Lost words:** words inside asterisks are deleted until `cleanReply` is fixed.
- **Not tested:** Mistral Small (the free key was rate limited on every call) and the Local AI, because no GPU job was allowed in
  this pass.
