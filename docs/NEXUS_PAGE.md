[size=4][b]Key features[/b][/size]
[b]Talk to anyone[/b][list]
[*][b]Voice or text.[/b] Face someone and press your talk key: hold it while you speak and let go to send, or type. They answer out loud with subtitles, and now and then ask you something back. The microphone opens the instant you press, so your first words are not lost. Voice activation (Settings > Voices, off by default) is hands-free.
[*][b]Key bindings and controller.[/b] Companion Manager > Key bindings gives each action a keyboard key, a combination such as Shift+F5, or a controller button, with no restart. Defaults: F7 talk by voice, F6 talk by text, F10 group voice chat, F8 group text chat, F4 the Companion Manager.
[*][b]Say a dialogue choice (off by default).[/b] Settings > Gameplay > Speak dialogue choices: say its words or its number to pick it.
[*][b]The game's named cast and the whole city.[/b] 126 named characters, from Panam, Johnny, Judy, Takemura, Kerry, River and Alt Cunningham on, each with a personality researched from the game's lore. Everyone else you can aim at (gangers, NCPD, guards, ripperdocs, vendors, bartenders, quest extras, passers-by) answers as their kind of person: 40 kinds with 80 personalities. Shopkeepers remember you, and each passer-by gets a fitting Night City name, shown on the scanner and in subtitles.
[*][b]Talk has consequences.[/b] Threaten or insult an ordinary person and they react from who they are: civilians run or call the police, gangers and guards fight back. Settings > Gameplay > NPCs react to threats and commands (off by default) adds commands such as "get down" and fistfights.
[*][b]Story characters where they stand.[/b] Settings > Conversations > Talk to story characters (off by default, reload your save): the game's own Judy, Jackie, Panam or Rogue answer in place, with voice and face only.
[*][b]Group chat.[/b] Two or three people at once, companions or anyone near you, taking turns. Nobody in front of you? Your party answers.
[*][b]Phone Chat and holocalls.[/b] Text any character with a profile here, your own characters included, from the Chat tab on V's phone (on a contact: T text, H call, J invite, L dismiss, F favorite), or call them and watch them pick up on screen.
[/list][b]Companions[/b][list]
[*][b]Invite almost anyone[/b] from Characters to invite, plus hundreds more character models from the Model browser. No party size limit. Favorites go first in the list and on the phone, and Companion Manager > Parties saves your party for one-press Spawn party.
[*][b]Works with character spawning mods.[/b] Characters spawned by other mods, Night City Allies followers among them, talk too, as themselves when they have a profile here, and Lucy and Rita Wheeler have their own voices and memory.
[*][b]They live alongside you.[/b] They follow through doors, lifts and fast travel, sit on couches and bar stools, ride in your car and fight with the game's own combat AI. At a calm moment they speak up, and two or more near chat among themselves (Settings > Conversations > Companions speak up). Ask one to drive ("can you drive us to the waypoint?") and they take the wheel.
[*][b]Orders and reactions.[/b] Tag an enemy with your scanner (or look at them) and tell them to attack, or to leave (or use Dismiss in Current party). They comment on fights and loot (Settings > Gameplay, on by default).
[*][b]Immersive invitations (off by default).[/b] Settings > Gameplay: inviting becomes an appointment. They come on foot or by car and text when they arrive.
[*][b]Housemates (off by default).[/b] Settings > Gameplay > Housemates: tell a character they can stay and they move into one of your apartments, then live there. The same page has the apartment, chat pause and Evict all housemates.
[*][b]Eddies with characters (off by default).[/b] Settings > Gameplay: borrow, lend, give or pay back eddies by talking. Characters decide from who they are and how they feel about you, and nothing leaves your wallet until you say yes.
[*][b]Revenge ambushes and outfit changes (both off by default).[/b] Settings > Gameplay: a gang you keep killing may rarely send a small squad (never mid-fight, indoors or while wanted), and companions change looks only while you are not looking.
[/list][b]Faces and voices[/b][list]
[*][b]Faces and bodies that act.[/b] Lips follow the words, the face shifts at the word the feeling changes, eyes and head follow you, and characters gesture, nod and hold poses with props.
[*][b]Voices that sound like them.[/b] Pocket TTS (default) runs on your processor with no setup, Voxtral 3 makes each voice in the cloud with a Mistral key, and OmniVoice (NVIDIA) can laugh and sigh. Natural speech adds pauses and hesitations. The companion and V volume sliders both run 50 to 300 percent. If a voice fails mid-reply, you still get the whole reply as text.
[*][b]V speaks too.[/b] Lines you type are voiced in a V voice that matches yours (Settings > Voices > V's voice for typed lines), and V's voice for microphone lines (off by default) does the same for spoken ones.
[*][b]OmniVoice on AMD Radeon (experimental).[/b] With the OmniVoice pack and a Radeon RX 6000, 7000 or 9000 card, Settings > Voices > OmniVoice for AMD downloads AMD's ROCm PyTorch (about 1.5 GB) and turns it on. The one-button setup is not yet tried on AMD hardware.
[*][b]OmniVoice on another PC.[/b] Settings > Voices > Custom voices > Remote OmniVoice: paste the address of an OmniVoice server (see Optional packs) and press Test. Nothing of OmniVoice loads on your game PC.
[*][b]Inworld voice (your own key).[/b] At the bottom of Settings > Voices > Custom voices, add an Inworld API key from [url=https://inworld.ai]inworld.ai[/url], press Test connection and pick Inworld as the last entry of Companion voices. Companions speak in cloned voices, English only. The free plan is a one-time trial of about 70 minutes. Not used with Mature content.
[/list][b]Story and memory[/b][list]
[*][b]They know the moment.[/b] They pick up from the game's own dialogue and know your quest, clothes and what you carry. With Companion vision on they also get a fresh glimpse of the game window (never saved), with any provider.
[*][b]Quest givers explain the job.[/b] Ask "what do I do next?" and they walk you through the step you are on, never beyond it. Covers 171 jobs, Phantom Liberty included.
[*][b]They remember you.[/b] Conversations stay with a character across saves, and Judy, Panam, River, Kerry and Meredith know where things stand with V. Make one character, or everyone, forget at any time.
[*][b]Johnny in your head (off by default).[/b] Give a key to Talk to Johnny by voice or text and he answers in V's head. With Speaks up rarely or often he also comments on people you meet, jobs you finish and romances.
[/list][b]Make it yours[/b][list]
[*][b]Character editor.[/b] Companion Manager > Home > Character editor opens in your browser: edit any character's bio, speaking style and memories, or make your own characters (Google Gemini, Local AI or Custom). The Look and moves tab sets outfit, gestures, idle moves and reply length, and the Voice tab gives a character a new voice from a short clip (see the FAQ).
[*][b]World rules.[/b] Write rules for all of Night City, for example "nobody knows V's name unless V says it", and every character follows them from the next reply.
[*][b]Prompting.[/b] The editor's Prompting tab tunes the prompt per AI option: output rules, example replies, sampling, length limits and an Advanced parameters box, with a live preview and a reset for any part. Game facts, safety rules and Mature hard limits stay locked. Convai's prompting lives on Convai's side.
[*][b]Content packs.[/b] Small no-code add-ons that change how characters talk (world rules, prompt wording, backstories, names, a separate memory). Extract into your Cyberpunk 2077 folder and switch on in Settings > Content packs. Your editor edits win over a pack. [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/35950][b]Medieval Night City - AI NPCs[/b][/url], a separate add-on page, is the first and turns Night City medieval.
[*][b]Choose what they know.[/b] Settings > Conversations > Dynamic info has twelve switches for what characters are told (Location, Time and weather, Health, V's money, V's gear and clothes, Quest and journal, Music and TV and more). Off means left out, on every provider.
[*][b]Bring your own AI and any language.[/b] Custom works with OpenRouter, Nano-GPT, Ollama, LM Studio, Mistral (a preset) or any OpenAI-compatible service, and Local AI runs your own model files (see Use your own AI model). Menus come in sixteen languages and characters can answer in yours (see Languages).
[/list][b]Light on your game[/b][list]
[*][b]Heavy work stays out of the game.[/b] It runs in a separate background service and the default setup uses no video memory. Unused models unload, and Local AI steps down to a lighter model when the game needs the memory.
[*][b]Fast replies.[/b] Everything between your words and the first sound runs at High Windows priority once you are in the world. If the game stutters, see Troubleshooting.

[size=4][b]Recommended settings[/b][/size]
Pick a provider in Companion Manager > Settings > Conversation provider. Local is free and private, cloud is the quick start.
[b]1. Local: free, no internet, no account, no limit[/b][list]
[*][b]You need[/b] a graphics card with room to spare next to the game: 12 GB of video memory or more (see How much video memory?), at least 16 GB of system memory and Vulkan (NVIDIA or AMD). It shares the card with the game, so it is slower than cloud on most PCs.
[*][b]Install[/b] a Local AI pack: [url=https://huggingface.co/alystria-ai/ai-npc-cyberpunk-mod-packs/blob/main/local-ai/LocalAI-Standard-Gemma-4-E4B.zip][b]Local AI standard[/b][/url], or [url=https://huggingface.co/alystria-ai/ai-npc-cyberpunk-mod-packs/blob/main/local-ai/LocalAI-Lite-Bonsai-2-4B.zip][b]Local AI lite[/b][/url] for smaller cards. A Mature pack works too. Extract it into your game folder like the mod, then choose [b]Local AI[/b] as the provider (with only a Mature pack, also turn on [b]Mature content[/b]). The mod starts and stops the AI by itself, with no server, Ollama, LM Studio or command line.
[*][b]Voice:[/b] [b]Pocket[/b] (included, English, on your processor), or [b]OmniVoice[/b] with its pack (see Optional packs) on an NVIDIA card with 12 GB or more free next to the game, for game-like voices and other languages.
[*][b]Local stays local:[/b] if your local voice or Local AI fails, you get a message or text only. The mod never switches to a cloud voice or cloud text on its own.
[/list][b]2. Cloud: fastest replies, light on your PC, no extra downloads[/b][list]
[*][b]Convai (higher tier):[/b] already set up, nothing to do. The most complete option: characters act on what you ask and see where you are. The free tier gives 500 turns a day and Settings shows how many are left.
[*][b]Google Gemini (free key):[/b] about a second to the first words. Make a free key at [url=https://aistudio.google.com/apikey]Google AI Studio[/url] (a Google account is enough, no card), choose [b]Google Gemini (free plan)[/b] in Settings > Conversation provider and press [b]Add[/b]. About 1,000 replies a day, up to 15 a minute. Not offered in every country, and Google may use free-plan chats to improve its products.
[*][b]Mistral:[/b] add a key with the [b]Mistral[/b] preset on the Custom page ([url=https://console.mistral.ai]console.mistral.ai[/url]). New accounts don't seem to get a free key any more.
[*][b]Voice:[/b] Pocket TTS runs on your processor with no limit. With a Mistral key, choose [b]Voxtral[/b] in Settings > Voices > Companion voices.
[/list]

[b]How much video memory?[/b]
The game uses roughly 6 to 7 GB at 1080p, 9 to 10 GB at 1440p or with ray tracing, 12 GB or more at 4K or path tracing. Local AI and OmniVoice need room on top:[list]
[*][b]8 GB:[/b] cloud text with Pocket or Voxtral is fastest. Local AI still starts, with the part that does not fit on your processor (slower). OmniVoice is not started: the mod uses Pocket and tells you once.
[*][b]10 to 12 GB:[/b] Local AI lite with Pocket at 1080p without ray tracing, or OmniVoice with cloud text.
[*][b]12 to 16 GB:[/b] Local AI standard with Pocket at 1080p, or lite with ray tracing or 1440p.
[*][b]16 GB or more:[/b] Local AI standard with ray tracing or 1440p and up, or Local AI with OmniVoice.
[/list]Added on top of the game (measured on a 12 GB RTX 4080 Laptop): cloud text with Pocket or Voxtral [b]0 GB[/b] (the default). Pocket with Local AI lite about 1.8 GB, with standard about 3.1 GB (needs about 6 GB free to start). OmniVoice with cloud text about 2 GB (up to 4 GB), with Local AI lite about 3.9 GB (up to 5.9 GB), with standard about 5.2 GB (up to 7.3 GB). If your card runs short, the mod steps down by itself (standard to lite, then part of the model on your processor, never to the cloud). Lowering texture quality frees the most room. Companion vision adds its vision file (see Optional packs): about 0.8 GB for standard, 0.4 GB for lite.

[b]Steps[/b][list=1]
[*]Install the mods in Requirements.
[*]Download [b]AI NPC LLM Companions[/b] from this page (the main file for game 2.5, or the optional file for 2.31). It holds everything.
[*]Close the game. Extract the zip into your [b]Cyberpunk 2077 folder[/b] (the one with bin, archive and r6) and let the folders merge. If Windows asks about files with the same names, choose Replace: it is always safe. Don't extract into a new folder named after the zip, as "Extract All" and 7-Zip's "Extract to" suggest. Extract it before the OmniVoice pack.
[*]Want Local AI or Mature? Extract its pack into the same folder the same way.
[*]Start the game (the first start takes a little longer), then check your keys under [b]Companion Manager > Key bindings[/b] or in Cyber Engine Tweaks' [b]Bindings[/b].

[b]Vortex[/b][list]
[*][b]Install:[/b] add the main file with Vortex's download button on this page, or drop its zip onto Vortex's Mods page, then click Deploy. It is the only mod you need. Vortex moving the HOW TO INSTALL file to its mod-extra-files folder, and the warning sign in its Version column, are harmless.
[*][b]Packs:[/b] for Local AI and Mature, Vortex shows "You Have Reached The Fallback Installer": choose "Yes, Install To Staging Anyway". Two packs report "unresolved file conflicts" because they share identical files, so load either one after the other. Extract the OmniVoice pack by hand, Vortex can't install it.
[*][b]Updating:[/b] add the newer main file the same way, choose "Replace the existing mod" (it may first ask to replace the file, say yes), then Deploy. If Vortex shows "Mod files were changed outside Vortex", click Save all changes (or Use newer file for all changes), never Revert, then Confirm.
[/list][b]Mod Organizer 2[/b][list]
[*][b]Install:[/b] add the main file as one mod. It holds everything.
[*][b]Updating:[/b] install the newer main file the same way and choose Merge if asked, so the packs in its folder stay.
[*][b]Packs:[/b] put the multilingual packs and the PocketTTS models INTO that mod's folder (replace files). The other packs can be mods of their own.
[/list][b]Coming from 0.3.3 or older, with a Scripts mod and a Runtime mod in Vortex or Mod Organizer 2?[/b]
Remove both of the old mods, then install the main file as one mod and deploy. From 0.3.4 the main file holds both, and the managers show three mods until you do. Your memories, keys and characters live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AiNpcCyberpunkMod, outside any mod, so none is lost. Packs you put inside the old Runtime mod's folder go with it, so put them into the new mod again.

[b]Which file, and updating[/b][list]
[*][b]New install, Vortex, Mod Organizer 2:[/b] the main file for your game version. Never the Update file with Vortex or Mod Organizer 2. Settings > Help shows the version of the Scripts and of the Runtime.
[*][b]Updating by hand:[/b] download the Update for your game from this page (named Update, then the oldest version it runs on, then the new one, in Updates for game 2.5 and in Optional files for 2.31). Settings, memories and packs are kept, so never delete the mod's folders to update.
[*][b]Too old for the Update?[/b] If Settings > Help says the Runtime is too old, extract the main file over your install. Moved from 2.31 to 2.5? Take the main file for 2.5 the same way.

[size=4][b]Use your own AI model or model server[/b][/size]
Local AI can use models you already run. Create a text file named [b]manifest.custom.json[/b] (check it does not end in .txt) in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AiNpcCyberpunkMod\local-ai\ (paste that into the Explorer address bar, and make the folder if needed). It survives reinstalls and mod managers. A setup in the older place, Cyberpunk 2077\red4ext\plugins\AiNpcCyberpunkMod\app\local-ai\, is still read but a clean reinstall deletes it, so copy it over.

[b]Your own model server[/b] (KoboldCpp, LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp, or anything OpenAI-compatible). Start the server first, then:
[code]{"version":1,"models":[{"endpoint":"http://127.0.0.1:5001/v1","name":"My Server"}]}[/code]
Usual addresses: KoboldCpp http://127.0.0.1:5001/v1, LM Studio http://127.0.0.1:1234/v1, Ollama http://127.0.0.1:11434/v1 (also add "model":"qwen3:8b", or the model you pulled). If your server wants a key, add "apiKey":"your-key". No Local AI pack is needed.

[b]Your own model file[/b] (any GGUF). Put it in a folder named custom next to the manifest, then:
[code]{"version":1,"models":[{"file":"custom/MyModel-Q4_K_M.gguf","name":"My Model"}]}[/code]
This runs on the mod's own engine, so no Local AI pack is needed.

Then in game: Settings > Conversation provider, choose Local AI and pick your model in the Local AI model row. List several models in the same file, separated by commas. Your model shares the graphics card with the game, so leave it enough video memory.

[size=4][b]FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]Will it slow my game down, or change the story?[/b]
No. The heavy work runs outside the game, and companions are temporary copies the mod owns. The game's story characters are left alone unless you turn on Talk to story characters.

[b]Can I run the AI on a second PC?[/b]
Yes. On the second PC (a GTX 1060 6 GB is enough) run llama.cpp's Vulkan server with the Local AI lite model and start it with [b]--host 0.0.0.0[/b]. Allow it through the Windows firewall on Private and Public networks. Then in the mod choose Custom, open the Custom endpoint page and paste [b]http://169.254.x.x:8080/v1 (its own address)[/b] and the model name you gave the server. A direct network cable works with no router (run ipconfig to read the 169.254 addresses). The exact command is in the README on GitHub: [url=https://github.com/alystria-ai/ai-npc-cyberpunk-mod#run-the-ai-on-another-pc]Run the AI on another PC[/url].

[b]Can I make my own characters?[/b]
Yes, with Google Gemini, Local AI or a Custom endpoint. Face a character with no profile here, open Settings > Edit characters > Open > New character. Give them a name, a bio, a way of speaking and optionally a voice clip. With Convai they talk as an ordinary person. Your characters show in Characters to invite and in the phone's Chat tab.

[b]Can I change a character's voice?[/b]
Yes. In the same editor, open a story character's Voice tab (or one of your own characters) and choose Add voice clip: a WAV, MP3, OGG or FLAC file of 5 to 20 seconds of one person speaking clearly, with the words typed out. Pocket, OmniVoice and Voxtral copy that voice from the character's next line, with no restart. Remove clip brings the original back, and the clip stays on your PC.

[size=4][b]Troubleshooting[/b][/size]
[b]A new game hangs on game 2.5.[/b]
That is Cyber Engine Tweaks 1.38.0, not this mod. Start the new game without it installed, save once, then install it again.

[b]The game stutters since the High priority.[/b]
Make a text file named [b]priority.txt[/b] in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AiNpcCyberpunkMod\data (paste that into the Explorer address bar) with one line, [b]priority = normal[/b], and restart the game. Delete the file to return to High.

[b]V never speaks my lines.[/b]
Typed lines are voiced by default (Settings > Voices > V's voice for typed lines). Microphone lines only with [b]V's voice for microphone lines[/b] on. If you type a line, the row is not Off and V stays silent, open Settings > Help > Help and support, press Generate report and send it to me.

[b]The Companion Manager doesn't show up.[/b]
Press your manager key (stand up first if V is sitting), or Esc, then Mods, and choose Companion Manager (in Native Settings UI, the AI NPC Cyberpunk Mod tab). No Mods entry? Install or update Mod Configuration Menu or Native Settings UI.

[b]Settings says it needs a browser, or the service does not start.[/b]
Install or repair Microsoft Edge (free from [url=https://www.microsoft.com/edge]microsoft.com/edge[/url]) or install Google Chrome, then restart the game. Browser installed somewhere unusual? Make a file named [b]browser.txt[/b] next to launcher.ini (red4ext\plugins\AiNpcCyberpunkMod) with the path to its exe on one line.

[b]Settings says "No model installed" for Local AI.[/b]
The pack was probably extracted into an extra folder named after the zip. Extract it again into the Cyberpunk 2077 folder itself. With only a Mature pack, turn on Mature content in Settings > Conversation provider.

[b]Settings > Help says Windows blocks the files of a voice or of Local AI.[/b]
Windows' Smart App Control (or a company's Application Control policy) refuses to load the files they need, so on that PC they cannot run. Choose a cloud voice such as Voxtral and a cloud provider, or send a Generate report from Settings > Help.

[b]I only get subtitles, or a voice error.[/b]
Open Settings > Help: it names the cause. With OmniVoice it is usually too little free video memory next to the game, so choose Pocket or Voxtral in Settings > Voices. If the notice mentions the paging file (error 1455), enlarge it or close other programs, then restart the game.

Post what you see (a screenshot helps) on this page's Posts tab or in the Discord server below.

In game, open Companion Manager > Settings > Help > Help and support, choose [b]Generate report[/b], and send that file with what you were doing.
