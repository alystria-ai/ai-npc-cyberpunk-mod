[size=4][b]How it runs[/b][/size]
Three ways, all free. Pick one in Companion Manager > Settings > Conversation provider.

[list]
[*][b]Local AI: fully on your own PC.[/b] Download a Local AI pack and your characters think and speak on your own computer. No account, no key, no internet and no limit. It runs on your graphics card (Vulkan, NVIDIA or AMD): the lite model uses about 1.8 GB of video memory, the standard model about 3.1 GB and needs about 6 GB free to start (with less, the lite model is used). It also needs at least 16 GB of system memory and uses about 5 GB more than usual while it runs.
[*][b]Mistral: free account, fastest cloud replies.[/b] Make a free Mistral account (it asks for phone verification), paste its key once, and get about 5,000 turns a day. Replies are plainer than Convai's. The same key unlocks Voxtral, a voice made in the cloud for each character.
[*][b]Convai free tier (the default): nothing to set up.[/b] Install, start the game and talk. It gives 100 turns a day, and Settings shows how many are left. Convai's paid plans give more turns, and their plan page is in the Convai settings.
[*][b]Voices.[/b] Pocket TTS runs on your processor and never counts against a daily limit. Voxtral needs a Mistral key. OmniVoice needs an NVIDIA card and its optional pack.
[/list]

[size=4][b]How much video memory? (quick guide)[/b][/size]
Pick a voice and a text option. Here is what each pair adds on top of the game itself:

[list]
[*]Pocket voice + cloud text (Convai or Mistral): 0 GB, the default
[*]Pocket voice + Local AI lite: about 1.8 GB
[*]Pocket voice + Local AI standard: about 3.1 GB
[*]OmniVoice + cloud text: about 2 GB (up to 4 GB)
[*]OmniVoice + Local AI lite: about 3.9 GB (up to 5.9 GB)
[*]OmniVoice + Local AI standard: about 5.2 GB (up to 7.3 GB)
[/list]

The Voxtral voice is like Pocket here: it runs in the cloud and uses no video memory. Measured on one 12 GB RTX 4080 Laptop; your game settings decide how much room is left. If your card runs short, the mod steps down by itself (standard to lite, then lite to cloud text), and you can change either choice in Settings at any time.

[size=4][b]Use your own AI model or model server[/b][/size]
Already run AI models on your PC? Local AI can use them instead of its own packs. All of this goes in the Local AI folder: Cyberpunk 2077\red4ext\plugins\AiNpcCyberpunkMod\app\local-ai\. Create a text file there named manifest.custom.json (check it does not end in .txt).

[b]Your own model server[/b] (KoboldCpp, LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp, or anything with an OpenAI-compatible API). Start the server first, then put its address in the file:
[code]{"version":1,"models":[{"endpoint":"http://127.0.0.1:5001/v1","name":"My Server"}]}[/code]
Usual addresses: KoboldCpp http://127.0.0.1:5001/v1, LM Studio http://127.0.0.1:1234/v1 (start its local server), Ollama http://127.0.0.1:11434/v1 (also add "model":"qwen3:8b", or whichever model you pulled). If your server asks for a password or key, add "apiKey":"your-key". No Local AI pack is needed for this.

[b]Your own model file[/b] (any GGUF). Put it in a folder named custom inside the Local AI folder, then:
[code]{"version":1,"models":[{"file":"custom/MyModel-Q4_K_M.gguf","name":"My Model"}]}[/code]
This runs on the mod's own engine, so a Local AI or Mature pack must be installed.

Then start the game, open Settings > Conversation provider, choose Local AI, pick your model in the Local AI model row and press Apply. You can list several models in the same file, separated by commas inside the brackets. Your server or model shares the graphics card with the game, so pick a size that leaves the game enough video memory.

[size=4][b]FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]Will it slow my game down?[/b]
The heavy work runs in a separate background service, not inside the game. The text and the voice are separate choices, and only OmniVoice and Local AI use your graphics card. Extra video memory on top of the game: none with cloud text (Convai or Mistral) and the Pocket or Voxtral voice, the default; about 1.8 GB with Local AI lite; about 3.1 GB with Local AI standard; about 2 GB (up to 4 GB) with OmniVoice; the two add up when combined. If the card runs short, the mod steps down by itself: standard to lite, then lite to cloud text.

[b]Do I have to set keys?[/b]
Yes. Open Cyber Engine Tweaks' Bindings and give a key to each AI NPC Cyberpunk Mod action, such as talk by text, talk by voice and the companion manager. The mod never assigns or overwrites keys for you.

[b]Does it change the story?[/b]
No. Companions are temporary copies that the mod owns. Story characters you meet in the world are left alone by default, so the story plays out as written. Talking to them is off until you turn on Settings > Conversations > Talk to story characters (then reload your save); they then answer with voice and face only.

[b]What leaves my computer?[/b]
With Convai, your lines go to Convai, along with a recent picture of only the game window to give character's vision input. That picture is taken only while someone you can talk to is near, kept in memory for a few seconds and never saved. With Mistral, your lines go to Mistral. With Local AI, nothing leaves your computer for replies.

[b]What languages can I use?[/b]
The menus come in sixteen languages. Turn on Multilingual conversations to talk in any language. Pocket and Voxtral speak English only, so those replies use OmniVoice, or Convai's cloud voice without the pack. The Convai microphone expects English, so type in other languages.

[b]The Companion Manager doesn't show up. What now?[/b]
It lives inside Mod Configuration Menu: press Esc and open MCM > Companion Manager (the manager key jumps there). If there is no MCM entry in the pause menu, install or update Mod Configuration Menu from the Requirements.

[b]The conversation service does not start, and Settings says it needs a browser. What now?[/b]
The voice service runs a hidden copy of a Chromium browser, and the mod ships none. It looks for Microsoft Edge first, then Google Chrome, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera and a few others. If a tweak tool removed Edge, or you have none of these, install or repair Microsoft Edge (free from [url=https://www.microsoft.com/edge]microsoft.com/edge[/url]) or install Google Chrome, then restart the game. Firefox cannot be used.

[b]Settings says "No model installed" for Local AI. What now?[/b]
The pack was most likely extracted into an extra folder named after the zip. Download the Runtime 0.2.1 again from Hugging Face, extract it into your Cyberpunk 2077 folder, and start the game: the mod moves the pack files into place by itself. Or move the red4ext folder from inside that extra folder up into the Cyberpunk 2077 folder yourself. With only a Mature pack, also turn on Mature content in Settings > Conversation provider. Still stuck? Generate a report (see below) and send it.
