[size=4][b]Key features[/b][/size]
[b]Talk to anyone[/b][list]
[*][b]Voice or text.[/b] Face someone and press your talk key: hold it while you speak and let go to send, or type. They answer out loud with subtitles, cinematic or above the speaker, and now and then ask you something back.
[*][b]Hold to talk, instantly.[/b] The microphone opens the moment you press the talk key, so your first words are not lost, and it stays ready for 15 seconds after a voice turn (after a pause the first press shows Opening microphone, then Speak now). For hands-free talk, turn on Voice activation in Settings > Voices (off by default). Microphone not heard? Pick the input in Settings > Voices > Microphone.
[*][b]Key bindings and controller.[/b] Companion Manager > Key bindings gives each action a keyboard key or controller button, with no restart. Set from the start: F7 talk by voice, F6 talk by text, F10 group voice chat, F8 group text chat and F4 the Companion Manager. Change, clear or restore them, or use a combination such as Shift+F5 (hold Shift, Ctrl or Alt while you press it). Johnny has his own talk keys there, with no key set at first. For a controller, give Talk by voice a button such as the left stick click, then hold it, speak and let go to send.
[*][b]Say a dialogue choice (off by default).[/b] Settings > Gameplay > Speak dialogue choices: while the game shows its choices, say one to pick it (its words, part of them, or its number, like "two" or "the last one"). If nothing fits clearly, nothing is picked and no character answers.
[*][b]Nobody in front of you? Your party answers.[/b] Press the talk key without facing anyone and the first member of your party answers. The group keys take the first plus two others.
[*][b]The game's named cast.[/b] 126 named characters, from Panam, Johnny, Judy, Takemura, Kerry, River and Alt Cunningham on, each with a personality researched from the game's own lore.
[*][b]The whole city.[/b] Gangers, NCPD officers, guards, ripperdocs, vendors, bartenders and passers-by (everyone you can aim at, quest extras included) answer as their kind of person: 40 kinds of people with 80 personalities (a woman's and a man's for each, MaxTac, NUSA and joytoys included). Shopkeepers you come back to remember you. Each nameless passer-by gets a Night City name that fits who they are (a Valentino gets a Latino name, a Tyger Claw a Japanese one), from about 13,000 names in 16 cultures, shown on the scanner and in the subtitles. Quest crowds such as the Afterlife patrons answer too, with a speaking face.
[*][b]Talk has consequences.[/b] Threaten or insult an ordinary person and they react from who they are and where you are: civilians run off or call the police, gangers, mercs and guards fight back. Settings > Gameplay > NPCs react to threats and commands (off by default) adds commands such as "get down", cowering and fistfights. Story and quest characters are never affected.
[*][b]Story characters where they stand.[/b] Settings > Conversations > Talk to story characters (off by default, then reload your save): the game's own Judy, Jackie, Panam or Rogue answer in place, with voice and face only. The story is left alone.
[*][b]Group chat.[/b] Talk to two or three people at once, your companions or anyone near you. They take turns and pick up from each other.
[*][b]Phone Chat and holocalls.[/b] Text any character that has a profile here, not only the game's contacts, from a Chat tab on V's phone (on a contact, T opens the text chat, H calls, J invites, L dismisses and F marks a favorite), or call them and watch them pick up live on screen. Characters you made yourself are in the Chat list too, and a chat opens with the cursor in the message box.
[*][b]Replies that sound like people.[/b] Fewer echoes of your question and stock closing lines, no reciting of where they are or the weather, and passers-by keep their own mood and conversation. Characters you look at or walk up to get their conversation ready in the background, so they answer straight away.
[/list][b]Companions[/b][list]
[*][b]Invite almost anyone[/b] from Characters to invite, plus hundreds more character models from the Model browser (a copy invited from there gets your character applied). With Mod Configuration Menu, type a name in its Search settings box to find a model at once, Rita Wheeler included. No party size limit.
[*][b]Favorites and saved parties.[/b] Add to favorites puts a character at the top of the list and first on the Chat tab of V's phone. Companion Manager > Parties > Save current party keeps your party as Party 1, Party 2 and so on, Spawn party invites everyone in it again with one press, and you can rename, update or delete a saved party.
[*][b]They live alongside you.[/b] They walk beside you, follow you through doors, lifts and fast travel, sit with you on couches, benches and bar stools, ride in your car and fight with the game's own combat AI. At a calm moment a companion also speaks up, and with two or more near they chat for up to three at a time and you can join in (Settings > Conversations > Companions speak up: Speaks up often by default, Off turns it off).
[*][b]Immersive invitations (off by default).[/b] Settings > Gameplay > Immersive invitations makes inviting a character an appointment: they come to you on foot or by car, text when they arrive and wait a while. Meet them on the way, or cancel with plain words like "never mind, cancel that". If the car cannot be used or no street is found, the character walks, and the game log says why.
[*][b]Housemates (off by default).[/b] Settings > Gameplay > Housemates: a character you tell they can stay at your place moves into one of your apartments, stays in your party until you tell them to leave, then lives there, stepping out of the way only during real scenes. The same page has Move-in apartment (Ask me, or one you own), Pause between chats (1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes), Housemates in one chat (2 to 4) and Evict all housemates.
[*][b]Revenge ambushes (off by default).[/b] Settings > Gameplay > Revenge ambushes: a gang you keep killing may, rarely, send a small squad after you. Never in a fight, a scene, a call, a vehicle, indoors, in a safe zone or while wanted.
[*][b]Companions change outfits (off by default).[/b] Settings > Gameplay > Companions change outfits: companions change into other looks, only while you are not looking.
[*][b]Ask them to drive.[/b] Set a waypoint, have your car nearby and say something like "can you drive us to the waypoint?". They take the wheel and you ride along, or tell you what is missing (no waypoint, your car not near or still moving).
[*][b]Eddies with characters (off by default).[/b] Settings > Gameplay > Eddies with characters: borrow, lend, give or pay back eddies just by talking. Each character has money of their own and decides from who they are and how they feel about you. A character may ask for a loan, but nothing leaves your wallet until you say yes in your own words. Characters the game placed, and passers-by, only talk about money.
[*][b]Orders and reactions.[/b] Tag an enemy with your scanner (or look right at them) and tell them to attack, or tell them to leave (or use Dismiss in Current party) to send them home. They comment when a fight starts and after it, and on the loot you pick up (Settings > Gameplay: React to battles and React to collected loot, both on by default). The game's own ambient barks of people nearby stay muted for the whole conversation.
[*][b]Night City Allies followers talk.[/b] NCA followers who are characters here talk as themselves, and Lucy (with Lucy for NCA) and Rita Wheeler have their own voices and memory. NCA keeps doing the following, fighting and driving.
[/list][b]Faces and voices[/b][list]
[*][b]Faces and bodies that act.[/b] Lips follow the words, the face changes at the very word where the feeling shifts, eyes and head follow you, and characters gesture, nod and hold idle poses with props such as a cigarette or a cup.
[*][b]Voices that sound like them.[/b] Pocket TTS (the default) runs on your processor with no setup, Voxtral 3 makes each voice in the cloud with a Mistral key (one steady voice per passer-by), and OmniVoice (NVIDIA) can laugh and sigh. Natural speech adds pauses and hesitations. If a voice fails after a reply has started, you still get the whole reply as text, even with Subtitles off.
[*][b]V speaks too.[/b] Lines you type are voiced in the V voice that matches yours (Settings > Voices > V's voice for typed lines: Off, Pocket, Voxtral or OmniVoice), and V's voice for microphone lines (off by default) does the same for the lines you speak. V uses one voice engine for both, and OmniVoice for V is offered only while your companions use OmniVoice. A mod that replaces V's voice? V's lines follow it, or give V your own clip in the character editor.
[*][b]OmniVoice on AMD Radeon (experimental).[/b] With the OmniVoice pack and an AMD Radeon RX 6000, 7000 or 9000 card, the Set up button in Settings > Voices > OmniVoice for AMD downloads AMD's ROCm PyTorch (about 1.5 GB) and turns OmniVoice on. One player runs it on a Radeon RX 9070 XT, but this one-button setup is not yet tried on AMD hardware. A MIOpen switch there tries the other convolution route if voices are slow.
[*][b]OmniVoice on another PC or graphics card.[/b] In Settings > Voices > Custom voices, turn on Remote OmniVoice, paste the address of an OmniVoice server (see Optional packs) and press Test. Nothing of OmniVoice then loads on your game PC, whichever local voice is chosen above, and if the server cannot be reached, replies show as text until it answers.
[*][b]Inworld voice (your own key).[/b] At the bottom of Settings > Voices > Custom voices, add an Inworld API key from [url=https://inworld.ai]inworld.ai[/url], press Test connection, then pick Inworld as the last entry of Companion voices. Each companion speaks in their own cloned voice, English only. Inworld's free plan is a one-time trial of about 70 minutes of speech, then pay as you go. It loads no voice model on your PC, it is not used with Mature content, and a reply it cannot deliver shows as text.
[/list][b]Story and memory[/b][list]
[*][b]They know the moment.[/b] They pick up from the game's own dialogue and know your quest, your clothes and what you carry. With Companions can see on they also get a fresh glimpse of the game window (never saved), with any provider.
[*][b]They notice what V wears, or does not.[/b] Companions and strangers see when V is topless or naked and read the Wardrobe outfit slot, and what V wears, holds and carries right now beats what was said earlier in the talk.
[*][b]Quest givers explain the job.[/b] Ask the character who gave you a job "what do I do next?" and they walk you through the step you are on, never beyond where you are. It covers 171 main jobs, side jobs and gigs, Phantom Liberty included.
[*][b]They know who you are to them.[/b] Strangers don't act as if they knew V, "I gave you eddies" is understood as a payment from V to them, and a companion knows which weapon they carry, also when it is holstered.
[*][b]They remember you.[/b] Conversations stay with a character across saves, and Judy, Panam, River, Kerry and Meredith know where things stand with V. Make one character, or everyone, forget at any time.
[*][b]Johnny in your head.[/b] Turn it on (off by default), give a key to Talk to Johnny by voice or by text, and Johnny answers in V's head wherever you look. He knows your jobs, his own history and how things stand between you, and with Speaks up rarely or Speaks up often he also comments on people you meet, jobs you finish and romances. He is only busy during real scenes, such as a cutscene with a choice or a call.
[/list][b]Make it yours[/b][list]
[*][b]Character editor.[/b] The Character editor button on Home in the Companion Manager opens it in your browser: edit any character's bio, speaking style and memories, or make characters of your own (Google Gemini, Local AI or Custom, see the FAQ). The Look and moves tab sets outfit, gestures, nods, idle moves, sitting, comments on fights and loot, and a shorter reply length.
[*][b]World rules.[/b] Write rules for the whole of Night City on the editor's World rules page, for example "nobody knows V's name unless V says it", and every character follows them from the next reply, on every provider.
[*][b]Prompting.[/b] The editor's Prompting tab tunes the prompt per AI option (Google Gemini, Local AI standard and lite, the Mature models, every Custom model): output rules, example replies, opening and closing lines, sampling (temperature, Top P, Top K, Min P, repeat, presence and frequency penalties), length limits and an Advanced parameters box (JSON, Local AI and Custom), with a live preview and a reset for any part. Where Mature is on, its wording is editable too, while the game facts, safety rules and Mature hard limits stay locked. Convai's prompting lives on Convai's side.
[*][b]Content packs.[/b] A small add-on of a settings file and some text, with no code, that changes how the characters talk: world rules, prompt wording, a character's backstory, style and name, name pools for passers-by, Convai twins and a separate memory. Extract its download into your Cyberpunk 2077 folder and switch it on in Settings > Content packs (names show after a restart, the rest from the next reply). Your own editor edits win over a pack. [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/35950][b]Medieval Night City - AI NPCs[/b][/url], a separate add-on page, is the first one and turns Night City into a medieval world, rebuilt from a custom lore bible.
[*][b]Tuned for each model.[/b] Mistral models, Local AI standard and lite and the Mature models each start from a prompt tuned and tested for them, with fewer echoes, lectures and off-character lines.
[*][b]Choose what they know.[/b] Settings > Conversations > Dynamic info has twelve switches for what characters are told: Location, Time and weather, Company and vehicle, Health, heat and fights, V's money, V's gear and clothes, V's gender and lifepath, Quest and journal, Recent game dialogue, Time since last talk, Music and TV (radio station, song and TV channel) and Other mods' state. Turn one off and it is left out, on every provider. Other mods' state is off by default: it reads Dark Future, the Equipment-EX outfit V wears and, on game 2.31 only, V's stamina, drink and injuries from Flesh and Chrome and Injuries (they do not run on game 2.5 yet).
[*][b]Voice clips.[/b] Give a character a new voice from a 5 to 20 second recording, applied without a restart (see the FAQ).
[*][b]Bring your own AI.[/b] The Custom provider works with OpenRouter, Nano-GPT, Ollama, LM Studio, Mistral (a preset) or any OpenAI-compatible service, and Local AI can run your own model files (see Use your own AI model). Switch provider in Settings and the very next line uses it. A prompt too big for a provider is trimmed to its limit and sent once more, and the limit is remembered.
[*][b]Any language.[/b] Menus in sixteen languages, and characters that answer in English, in the language you write in, or in a fixed language (see Languages).
[/list][b]Light on your game[/b][list]
[*][b]Heavy work stays out of the game.[/b] It runs in a separate background service, and the default setup uses no video memory. Unused models unload on their own, Local AI steps down to a lighter model when the game needs the memory (on cards from 12 to 24 GB this leaves more room for the game), and loading a Local AI model does not freeze the game.
[*][b]Fast replies.[/b] Everything between your words and the first sound of the reply (the background service, the voice workers, the Local AI server, speech recognition and the audio browser) runs at High Windows priority once you are in the world, so a busy game cannot make a reply wait. Before that it stays at normal priority, so the title screen does not stall. If the game stutters, see Troubleshooting.
[*][b]Any install.[/b] Works with Vortex, Mod Organizer 2 or a manual install.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Recommended settings[/b][/size]
Pick a conversation provider in Companion Manager > Settings > Conversation provider. Cloud is the easy start, Local runs on your own PC.
[b]1. Cloud: the best option[/b] (fastest replies, light on your PC, no extra downloads)[list]
[*][b]Convai (best):[/b] already set up, nothing to do. It is the most complete: characters act on what you ask and see where you are. The free tier gives 500 turns a day and Settings shows how many are left. A dropped connection is retried once by itself before the reply starts.
[*][b]Google Gemini (free plan):[/b] the free cloud option with fast replies, about a second to the first words, and light on your PC. Make a free key at [url=https://aistudio.google.com/apikey]Google AI Studio[/url] (a Google account is enough, no card), choose [b]Google Gemini (free plan)[/b] in Settings > Conversation provider, press [b]Add[/b] and you are done. About 1,000 replies a day, up to 15 a minute (Google sets the exact number). It is not offered in every country, and Google may use free-plan chats to improve its products.
[*][b]Mistral:[/b] used to give everyone a free API key with about 5,000 turns a day, but new accounts don't seem to get one. If you have a key, add it with the [b]Mistral[/b] preset on the Custom page ([url=https://console.mistral.ai]console.mistral.ai[/url]).
[*][b]Voice:[/b] Pocket TTS (the default) runs on your processor with no limit. With a Mistral key, choose [b]Voxtral[/b] in Settings > Voices > Companion voices for voices closest to the characters. If Voxtral cannot speak (no key, a limit, a Mistral plan that does not include it), replies show as text until it can.
[/list][b]2. Local: no internet, no account, no limit, nothing to set up[/b][list]
[*][b]You need[/b] a graphics card with room to spare next to the game: 12 GB of video memory or more (see How much video memory? below), at least 16 GB of system memory, and Vulkan (NVIDIA or AMD). It shares the card with the game, so it is slower than the cloud on most PCs.
[*][b]Install[/b] a Local AI pack: [url=https://huggingface.co/alystria-ai/ai-npc-cyberpunk-mod-packs/blob/main/local-ai/LocalAI-Standard-Gemma-4-E4B.zip][b]Local AI standard[/b][/url], or [url=https://huggingface.co/alystria-ai/ai-npc-cyberpunk-mod-packs/blob/main/local-ai/LocalAI-Lite-Bonsai-2-4B.zip][b]Local AI lite[/b][/url] for smaller graphics cards. A Mature pack works too. Extract it into your game folder like the mod: that is all. The mod starts the AI, loads the model and stops it by itself, with no server, no Ollama or LM Studio and no command line.
[*][b]Choose[/b] [b]Local AI[/b] as the provider. With only a Mature pack, also turn on [b]Mature content[/b] (Settings > Conversation provider).
[*][b]Voice:[/b] [b]Pocket[/b] (included, English, free, on your processor), or [b]OmniVoice[/b] with its pack (see Optional packs) on an NVIDIA card with 12 GB of video memory or more free next to the game, for voices like the game characters and other languages. With less, pick Pocket or Voxtral.
[*][b]Local stays local:[/b] if your local voice or Local AI fails, you get a message or text only. The mod never switches to a cloud voice or cloud text on its own.
[*][b]Your own models:[/b] already run KoboldCpp, LM Studio or Ollama, or have your own GGUF file? The mod can use them: see [b]Use your own AI model or model server[/b] below.
[/list]

[b]How much video memory?[/b]
The game itself uses most of the card: roughly 6 to 7 GB at 1080p, 9 to 10 GB at 1440p or with ray tracing, and 12 GB or more at 4K or with path tracing. Local AI and OmniVoice need room on top of that:[list]
[*][b]8 GB card:[/b] cloud text (Convai or Google Gemini) with Pocket or Voxtral is the fastest. Local AI still starts, with whatever does not fit next to the game running on your processor, so replies are slower (the mod says so once). OmniVoice is not started on a card this small: the mod uses Pocket and tells you once. Wherever OmniVoice runs short of free video memory, Pocket or Voxtral is the safe choice.
[*][b]10 to 12 GB:[/b] Local AI lite with Pocket at 1080p without ray tracing, or OmniVoice with cloud text.
[*][b]12 to 16 GB:[/b] Local AI standard with Pocket at 1080p, or lite with ray tracing or 1440p.
[*][b]16 GB or more:[/b] Local AI standard with ray tracing or 1440p and up, or Local AI with OmniVoice.
[/list]What each voice + text pair adds on top of the game:[list]
[*]Pocket or Voxtral voice + Convai or Google Gemini: [b]0 GB[/b] (the default)
[*]Pocket voice + Local AI lite: about 1.8 GB
[*]Pocket voice + Local AI standard: about 3.1 GB (needs about 6 GB free to start)
[*]OmniVoice + Convai or Google Gemini: about 2 GB (up to 4 GB)
[*]OmniVoice + Local AI lite: about 3.9 GB (up to 5.9 GB)
[*]OmniVoice + Local AI standard: about 5.2 GB (up to 7.3 GB)
[/list]Measured on a 12 GB RTX 4080 Laptop. If your card runs short, the mod steps down by itself (standard to lite, then part of the model on your processor, never to the cloud). Lowering texture quality in the game frees the most room. With Companions can see on, Local AI also loads its vision file (see Optional packs): about 0.8 GB more video memory for standard and 0.4 GB more for lite.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[b]Requirements[/b][list]
[*][b]Game and system:[/b] Cyberpunk 2077 version 2.5 (or the older 2.31) on Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). The file you install must be the build for your game version.
[*][b]Mods to install first, game 2.5:[/b] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/107]Cyber Engine Tweaks[/url] 1.38.0 (it can hang a NEW game, see Troubleshooting), [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/1511]redscript[/url] 0.5.32, [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/2380]RED4ext[/url] 1.31.0 or newer, [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/7780]Codeware[/url] 1.21.0, and either [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/31640]Mod Configuration Menu[/url] 0.10.5 or newer or [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/3518]Native Settings UI[/url] 1.97 (one of the two is enough, and with both the manager uses Mod Configuration Menu).
[*][b]Game 2.31 needs other versions:[/b] Cyber Engine Tweaks 1.37.1, redscript 0.5.31, RED4ext 1.30.0 (1.31.0 only starts on 2.5), the official [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/7780]Codeware[/url] 1.20.5, Mod Configuration Menu 0.10.5 or Native Settings UI 1.96. Codeware 1.21.0 is for 2.5 only.
[*][b]Not needed:[/b] Audioware and TweakXL. Voices play through the mod's own audio, so game patches that break Audioware cannot silence the mod. Having them does no harm.
[*][b]Browser:[/b] a Chromium browser such as Microsoft Edge (Windows 10 and 11 include it), Google Chrome, Brave or Opera. The voice service runs a hidden copy of it. Firefox does not work.
[*][b]Disk space:[/b] about 3 GB free, plus more for the optional packs. The Local AI and Mature packs need the Runtime of version 0.2.7 or newer.
[*][b]Internet:[/b] for Convai, Google Gemini or the Voxtral voice (Voxtral needs a Mistral key). Local AI with a local voice needs none.
[*][b]Graphics card:[/b] not needed for the default setup. Only OmniVoice and Local AI use one.
[/list]

[b]Steps[/b][list=1]
[*]Install the mods in Requirements.
[*]Download [b]AI NPC LLM Companions[/b] from this page (the main file for game 2.5, or the optional file for the older 2.31, by hand). It holds everything, so there is nothing else to download.
[*]Close the game. Extract the zip into your [b]Cyberpunk 2077 folder[/b] (the one with bin, archive and r6) and let the folders merge. If Windows asks about files with the same names, choose Replace: it is always safe. Don't extract into a new folder named after the zip: Windows' "Extract All" and 7-Zip's "Extract to" suggest one. Extract it before the OmniVoice pack.
[*]Want Local AI or Mature? Extract its pack into the same Cyberpunk 2077 folder the same way. That is all: the mod runs the model itself, no server or other app.
[*]Start the game. The first start takes a little longer while the voice service prepares itself.
[*]Check the keys (defaults under Key bindings in Key features) in game under [b]Companion Manager > Key bindings[/b] or in Cyber Engine Tweaks' [b]Bindings[/b]. A key you set before stays as it was.
[/list]

[b]Vortex[/b][list]
[*][b]Install:[/b] add the main file, [b]AI NPC LLM Companions[/b], with Vortex's download button on this page, or drop its zip onto Vortex's Mods page, then click Deploy. It holds everything, so it is the only mod you need. Vortex moves only the HOW TO INSTALL text file to its mod-extra-files folder, and the warning sign in its Version column only means the mod was added from a file. Both are harmless.
[*][b]Local AI and Mature packs:[/b] Vortex shows "You Have Reached The Fallback Installer". Choose "Yes, Install To Staging Anyway" and every file lands correctly. With two of these packs Vortex reports "unresolved file conflicts" because they share identical files (the licences and the embedding model). Pick either one to load after the other, it makes no difference.
[*][b]OmniVoice pack:[/b] extract it into the game folder by hand. Vortex can't install it, because it holds DLLs.
[*][b]Updating:[/b] download the newer main file and add it the same way. When Vortex asks, choose "Replace the existing mod" (it may first ask to replace the file, say yes), then click Deploy.
[*][b]External Changes:[/b] if Vortex shows "Mod files were changed outside Vortex" for this mod after an update, click Save all changes (or Use newer file for all changes) so it keeps the newer files, never Revert, then Confirm.
[/list][b]Mod Organizer 2[/b][list]
[*][b]Install:[/b] add the main file, [b]AI NPC LLM Companions[/b], as one mod. It holds everything.
[*][b]Updating:[/b] install the newer main file the same way and choose Merge if Mod Organizer 2 asks, so the packs you put in its folder stay.
[*][b]Packs:[/b] put the multilingual packs and the PocketTTS models INTO that mod's folder (replace files), not into mods of their own. The other packs can be mods of their own.
[/list][b]Coming from 0.3.3 or older, with a Scripts mod and a Runtime mod in Vortex or Mod Organizer 2?[/b]
Remove both of the old mods, then install the main file as one mod and deploy. From 0.3.4 the main file holds both, and the managers cannot know that it replaces the old two, so they show three mods until you do. Your memories, saved keys and characters are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AiNpcCyberpunkMod, outside any mod, and your settings are copied there and put back at the first start, so none is lost. Anything you put inside the old Runtime mod's folder (the multilingual packs, for example) goes with it, so put it into the new mod again.

[b]Which file, and updating[/b][list]
[*][b]New install, Vortex, Mod Organizer 2:[/b] the main file. Both files must be for your game version (2.5 or 2.31), and Settings > Help shows the version of the Scripts and of the Runtime.
[*][b]Updating by hand:[/b] download the Update for your game from this page (named Update, then the oldest version it runs on, then the new one, in the Updates section for game 2.5 and in Optional files for 2.31). [size=4][b]Use your own AI model or model server[/b][/size]
Already run AI models on your PC? Local AI can use them. Create a text file named [b]manifest.custom.json[/b] (check it does not end in .txt) in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AiNpcCyberpunkMod\local-ai\ (paste that into the Explorer address bar and make the local-ai folder if it is not there). It is outside the game folder, so it survives reinstalls and mod managers. The older place, Cyberpunk 2077\red4ext\plugins\AiNpcCyberpunkMod\app\local-ai\, still works and both are read, but a clean reinstall deletes it: to keep a setup you made there, copy its custom folder and manifest.custom.json into the %LOCALAPPDATA% folder.

[b]Your own model server[/b] (KoboldCpp, LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp, or anything with an OpenAI-compatible API). Start the server first, then:
[code]{"version":1,"models":[{"endpoint":"http://127.0.0.1:5001/v1","name":"My Server"}]}[/code]
Usual addresses: KoboldCpp http://127.0.0.1:5001/v1, LM Studio http://127.0.0.1:1234/v1 (start its local server), Ollama http://127.0.0.1:11434/v1 (also add "model":"qwen3:8b", or whichever model you pulled). If your server wants a password or key, add "apiKey":"your-key". No Local AI pack is needed.

[b]Your own model file[/b] (any GGUF). Put it in a folder named custom next to the manifest, then:
[code]{"version":1,"models":[{"file":"custom/MyModel-Q4_K_M.gguf","name":"My Model"}]}[/code]
This runs on the mod's own engine, which ships in the Runtime, so no Local AI pack is needed.

Then in game: Settings > Conversation provider, choose Local AI, pick your model in the Local AI model row. List several models in the same file, separated by commas. Your model shares the graphics card with the game, so pick a size that leaves the game enough video memory.

[size=4][b]FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]Will it slow my game down?[/b]
No. The heavy work runs outside the game and the default setup uses no video memory. See Light on your game and the video memory section above.

[b]Does it change the story?[/b]
No. Companions are temporary copies that the mod owns. The game's own story characters are left alone unless you turn on Talk to story characters (see Key features).

[b]Can I run the AI on a second PC?[/b]
Yes. On the second PC (a GTX 1060 6 GB is enough, and it needs no CUDA files) run llama.cpp's server, the Vulkan build, with the Local AI lite model, and start it with [b]--host 0.0.0.0[/b]. Allow it through the Windows firewall on Private and Public networks. Then in the mod choose Custom, open the Custom endpoint page and paste [b]http://169.254.x.x:8080/v1 (its own address)[/b] and the model name you gave the server. A direct network cable works with no router, and Windows gives both PCs 169.254 addresses by itself (run ipconfig to read them). The A to Z guide, with the exact command, is in the README on GitHub: [url=https://github.com/alystria-ai/ai-npc-cyberpunk-mod#run-the-ai-on-another-pc]Run the AI on another PC[/url].

[b]Can I make my own characters?[/b]
Yes, with Google Gemini, Local AI or a Custom endpoint. Face a character that has no profile here (for example a Night City Allies follower from a character mod), open Settings > Edit characters > Open and choose New character in the page that opens. Give them a name, a bio and a way of speaking. The page can fill in how the game recognises them from the character you are facing. An optional voice clip gives them a voice of their own. With Convai they talk as an ordinary person, and the game says so once. Your characters are listed in Characters to invite and in the Chat tab of V's phone.

[b]Can I change a character's voice?[/b]
Yes. In the same editor page, open a story character's Voice tab (or one of your own characters) and choose Add voice clip: a WAV, MP3, OGG or FLAC file of 5 to 20 seconds of one person speaking clearly, with the words said in it typed out. Pocket, OmniVoice and Voxtral copy that voice from that character's next line, with no restart. Remove clip brings the original voice back. The clip stays on your PC.

[size=4][b]Troubleshooting[/b][/size]
[b]A new game hangs on game 2.5.[/b]
That is Cyber Engine Tweaks 1.38.0, not this mod. Start the new game without Cyber Engine Tweaks installed, save once, then install it again.

[b]The game stutters since the High priority.[/b]
Make a text file named [b]priority.txt[/b] in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AiNpcCyberpunkMod\data (paste that into the Explorer address bar) with one line, [b]priority = normal[/b], and restart the game. That puts everything back to normal priority. Delete the file to return to High.

[b]Vortex or Mod Organizer 2 shows three mods.[/b]
You updated from the separate Scripts and Runtime files. See Coming from 0.3.3 or older under Install.

[b]V never speaks my lines.[/b]
Lines you type are voiced by default (Settings > Voices > V's voice for typed lines, Pocket). Lines you speak by microphone are not repeated unless [b]V's voice for microphone lines[/b] is on, and it is off by default. If you type a line, the row is not Off and V stays silent, open Settings > Help > Help and support, press Generate report and send it to me.

[b]The Companion Manager doesn't show up.[/b]
Press your manager key (stand up first if V is sitting), or press Esc, then Mods, and choose Companion Manager (in Native Settings UI, the AI NPC Cyberpunk Mod tab). No Mods entry? Install or update Mod Configuration Menu (0.10.5 or newer) or Native Settings UI.

[b]Settings says it needs a browser, or the service does not start.[/b]
Install or repair Microsoft Edge (free from [url=https://www.microsoft.com/edge]microsoft.com/edge[/url]) or install Google Chrome, then restart the game. Some Windows tweak tools remove Edge. Browser installed somewhere unusual? Make a file named [b]browser.txt[/b] next to launcher.ini (red4ext\plugins\AiNpcCyberpunkMod) with the path to its exe on one line.

[b]Settings says "No model installed" for Local AI.[/b]
The pack was probably extracted into an extra folder named after the zip. Extract it again into the Cyberpunk 2077 folder itself (the mod also moves misplaced packs into place at the next start). With only a Mature pack, turn on Mature content in Settings > Conversation provider.

[b]Settings > Help says Windows blocks the files of a voice or of Local AI.[/b]
Windows' Smart App Control (or a company's or school's Application Control policy) refuses to load the Python or model server files that the voice or Local AI needs, so on that PC they cannot run. Choose a cloud voice such as Voxtral and a cloud conversation provider, or send a Generate report from Settings > Help.

[b]I only get subtitles, or a voice error.[/b]
Open Settings > Help: it names the cause. With OmniVoice the usual cause is too little free video memory next to the game: choose Pocket or Voxtral (with a Mistral key) in Settings > Voices.

[b]A voice fails and the notice mentions Windows memory or the paging file.[/b]
Windows ran out of memory it could promise the voice (error 1455, the paging file is too small). The mod says so once, and Pocket takes over or the reply shows as text. Enlarge the paging file or close other programs, then restart the game.

[b]The mod does not load, or the manager will not open.[/b]
Post what you see (a screenshot helps) on this page's Posts tab or in the Discord server below.

[b]Something else?[/b]
In game, open Companion Manager > Settings > Help > Help and support, choose [b]Generate report[/b], and send that file with what you were doing.
