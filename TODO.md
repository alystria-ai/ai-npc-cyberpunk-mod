**2026-10-07: Convai early audio-player open (latency item A)**
- Where the time went (real Edge, real `realtime-client`, player, worklet, host pump and `ConvaiContinuousSpeech`; fake Convai SDK emitting 24 kHz audio at 2.87x with 100 ms chunks; simulated game finding the reply within 0 to 100 ms like `mod/init.lua`'s 0.1 s poll, then refreshing control every 40 ms; harness is scratch, not committed): the player was created at the first audio packet, and a fresh AudioContext+worklet only starts rendering about 120 to 230 ms after `open()` returns (about 20 ms of `open()` on a warm page, about 190 ms on a page's first context). Control to sound was 120 to 230 ms right after open, but about 30 ms when the context had been running for 300 ms or more. The 400 ms start-up buffer fills in about 105 ms at 2.87x, so it is not the bottleneck.
- Why the old note was too pessimistic: the bridge pump already sends a quiet control every 40 ms while the request is in flight, but a player opened early still must not depend on that (a bridge stall of 2 s would fail a turn that today is harmless), so the early player is not leased at all.
- Design: `ContinuousPcmPlayer.open({standby: true})` opens the device at turn start (`attachContinuous`, so text turns and the microphone finish) in the expected 24 kHz mono. A standby core renders silence, ignores controls and `hold`, rejects audio, and has no lease; it is bounded only by a 70 s ceiling (`PCM_LIMITS.standbyMs`, above the 60 s reply ceiling). `arm()` ends standby and starts the ordinary 2 s lease at that moment (it admits nothing; the game's allowing control still does). The client keeps the standby player separate from `live.player`, so a failed or unusable standby (open error, device change, a source format other than 24 kHz mono) just falls back to opening a player at the first audio as before. Cancel (`close`), a failed text send, a failed microphone finish and the 60 s no-audio timeout all stop it. Game ownership, the lease after the first audio and the Lua side are unchanged.
- Measured (3 interleaved before/after runs, think time 2.3 s, medians of per-run medians, first packet to first rendered samples): warm page 302 to 141 ms, first turn in a page 274 to 135 ms (to device-clock position above zero: 329 to 162 and 306 to 158). Control to sound fell from 120-230 ms to 35-65 ms. A 6 s think time also plays. Numbers move with machine load; the direction held in every run.
