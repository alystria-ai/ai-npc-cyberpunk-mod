### Pocket voice model (Kyutai)

The Pocket voice model in `app/models/pocket/` is built from Kyutai's published Pocket TTS export
(`kyutai/pocket-tts`). What the sources say:

- The code (github.com/kyutai-labs/pocket-tts) is MIT-licensed, including at the pinned revision.
- The Hugging Face model card for `kyutai/pocket-tts` declares the weights CC-BY-4.0. The
  repository is gated: access requires accepting the card's prohibited-use terms, which list voice
  impersonation or cloning without explicit and lawful consent among the prohibited uses.
- Per `receipt.json` and `decoder-receipt.json`, the shipped export takes its Mimi codec,
  `inference.yaml` and `tokenizer.json` from the template export, replaces the flow language-model
  tensors with weights trained for this mod, and replaces 44 Mimi decoder tensors with a retrained
  decoder. CC-BY-4.0 asks for attribution to Kyutai, a link to the licence
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and an indication that changes were made. This
  section is that notice: the Pocket voice model is adapted from Kyutai's Pocket TTS.
