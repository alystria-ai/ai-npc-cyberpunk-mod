## Description

**For adults only (18+).**

Cyberpunk Convai lets you talk to Cyberpunk 2077 characters, with companions who answer in their own voices. This add-on is a separate download for adults. It adds one setting, **Mature content**, and the uncensored models that make it work. Without this pack the main mod has no such setting.

**What it adds**

- A **Mature content** setting in Settings > Development, under Conversation provider. Off by default. Turning it on shows a one-line 18+ confirmation.
- Uncensored models for **Local AI**, running on your own graphics card. The standard Local AI models are trained to refuse most adult content, so the full effect needs these.
- Strong language, graphic violence, drug and cyberware-abuse talk, dark and morally grey themes, crude humour, and flirting, romance and sexual content between consenting adults.

**What does not change**

- Characters stay themselves: personalities, boundaries and orientations apply, and they can say no. Romance follows the relationship your save has with them.
- The **hard limits are always on**: no sexual content involving minors or anyone who seems under 18, none without consent, no bestiality or incest, nothing sexual about real people, no real-world instructions for weapons, explosives or drug synthesis, no hateful content about real protected groups. The mod holds them in a rule at the start of every conversation and in a check on every sentence before it is spoken.
- It applies to **Local AI only**. Convai, Mistral and Groq never get it.

**Requirements**

- You are 18 or older.
- Cyberpunk Convai (the main mod, Scripts and Runtime, same version) and its requirements.
- A Local AI pack (standard or lite) from the main mod's release page, working on your computer.
- About PLACEHOLDER GB of free disk space.

**Install**

1. Close the game.
2. Extract every part of this pack into your Cyberpunk 2077 folder, allowing folders to merge.
3. Start the game, open the Companion Manager, then Settings > Development.
4. Choose Local in Conversation provider and Apply, then turn on Mature content and Apply.

To turn it off, switch the setting off. To remove the pack, delete `manifest.mature.json` from the Local AI folder (and the model files listed in `mature/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`); the setting disappears and stays off.
